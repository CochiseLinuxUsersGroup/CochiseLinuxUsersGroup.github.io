Supported Linux Distributions

Free install & troubleshooting help in Sierra Vista, AZ

Officially Supported at Workshops & Meetings

At our mini-workshops, CLUG meetings, Ubuntu Hours, and other workshop events, we officially support and can assist with installing and troubleshooting the following distributions:

Fully Supported by Us

  • Ubuntu (all flavors)
  • Linux Mint (all flavors)
  • LMDE (Linux Mint Debian Edition)

We are happy to offer limited guidance for installing other Linux distributions if you bring your own installation media.

Note:
Support for any distributions not listed above is not guaranteed and should not be expected. This support list is based on what most of our members actively use and are best able to troubleshoot.
