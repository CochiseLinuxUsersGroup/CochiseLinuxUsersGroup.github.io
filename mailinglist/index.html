Mailing List Instructions

Stay up to date with meeting dates or ask the group a question between meetings — join the mailing list in two easy steps:

  1. Send an email to cochiselinux-request@freelists.org with subscribe in the subject. You'll receive a confirmation email.
  2. Click the URL in that email and follow the instructions — you're subscribed.

To post to the whole group, email cochiselinux@freelists.org. Use it between meetings to ask for help, share tips, announce events, or discuss Linux and FOSS. Archives are at freelists.org/archive/cochiselinux.

Privacy: Freelists only uses your address to deliver list mail. Unsubscribe anytime via the same confirmation link.
