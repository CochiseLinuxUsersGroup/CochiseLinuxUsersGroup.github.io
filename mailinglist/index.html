      Join the Mailing List

      Low-traffic · meeting reminders & quick help between meetups

      How to Join — 2 Steps

      1. Click Send Subscribe Email — or email cochiselinux-request@freelists.org with subscribe in the subject. You'll get a confirmation email.
      2. Click the URL in that email and follow the instructions — you're in.
        Don't see it? Check spam and whitelist @freelists.org.

      To post to everyone, email cochiselinux@freelists.org.

      What You'll Get

      • Meeting reminders + date/time changes for CLUG & Ubuntu Hour
      • Quick help between meetings — installs, Wi-Fi, printers, everyday use from 40+ members
      • Community — share tips, articles, and event announcements
      • Archive — browse past discussions

      Privacy: Freelists only uses your address for list mail. Volume ~2–8 msgs/month, no spam. Unsubscribe anytime via the same link.
