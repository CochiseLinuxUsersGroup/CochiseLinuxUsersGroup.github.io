CLUG Installfest - 20 January 2018

From Rex Bouwense · Jan 16, 2018

The Cochise Linux User Group will be conducting its first Installfest of
2018 on 20 January 1000-1600 at the University of Arizona South Learning
Resource Center, 1140 Colombo Ave, Sierra Vista AZ. Please attend if
you can even if it is just to stop by to say hello. We are still in
need of individuals to assist in installation. If you can help please
contact Rex Bouwense. If you are hesitant about installing there are
other things that you can do at the Installfest to insure its success.

Rex Bouwense
