CLUG Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Feb 17, 2020

The Cochise Linux User Group will hold its monthly meeting on
Saturday, 22 February 2020, at 1300 in the Sierra Vista Public Library
conference room, 2600 E. Tacoma Street, Sierra Vista AZ. There will be a
presentation on "Robo Linux" by Dr. Tim Kirk, the driving force behind
the founding and running of the Warrior Healing Center, the one stop
shop for veterans and their dependents who are having problems, here in
Sierra Vista and in Cochise County. Lets have a good showing for this
presentation. Attend if you can and bring a family member, a friend, or
a co-worker to introduce them to other aspects of Linux Operating Systems.

Rex
