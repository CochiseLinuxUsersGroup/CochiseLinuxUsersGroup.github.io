Meeting Presentation Updates

From Rex Bouwense · Feb 12, 2018

I have scheduled presentations for the next two Saturday CLUG meetings
at the SVPL conference room. On the 24th of February we will have a
video on "The Beginner's Guide to the Bash Terminal". This is designed
to give you a basic start on how to use the terminal emulator that is
installed on your computer. David Garcia and I have both viewed it and
highly recommend that you bring your laptop to follow along with the
presentation rather than merely listen to the speaker. This will enable
you to better understand what he is trying to teach you. On the 24th of
March we will have Chadwick Wheeler repeat his presentation on
Penetration Testing that was given to the University of Arizona South.
Put both of these meetings on your calendar and be sure that you attend.

Rex Bouwense
