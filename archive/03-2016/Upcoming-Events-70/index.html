Up-coming Events

From MajB · Mar 21, 2016

Just a friendly reminder that there is a Cochise Linux User Group
meeting on Saturday, 26 March 2016 at the Sierra Vista public library
2-3:45 pm. I have made arrangements for a financial adviser to give us
a short presentation (15-20 minutes). In addition to our regular
meeting we have to finalize plans for our participation in the 11th
annual West End Fair in Sierra Vista. Please attend if you can.
Other events that are scheduled are:
23 March, 5-6:30 pm Free Lecture and Demonstration on the Raspberry Pi
at the UA South Continuing Education Public Meeting Room, in Groth Hall,
1140 N. Colombo Ave, Sierra Vista AZ. Pizza will be served. See
attachment. Please RSVP to Dr. John DeLala if you plan to attend.
24 March, 6-7 pm Ubuntu Hour at the Schlozsky's Cafe, 3900 Fry Blvd,
Sierra Vista AZ hosted by Rex Bouwense.
A date to put on your calendar for the future is 14 May. Ubuntu
Arizona Local Community Team is sponsoring a Release Party for the new
version of Ubuntu (Xenial Xerus 16.04 LTS). It will be held at 5831 W.
Kesler St, Chandler AZ from 2pm until it is over. Wireless Internet
will be available if you want to install the new version or if you
already have it installed, bring your laptop to show it off. If you plan
to attend please RSVP to questions@azloco.com with a number that will be
attending. Hamburgers and hot dogs will be served. Additional
information can be found at http://www.azloco.org/node/235. Plan to
attend for a good time.
Rex Bouwense
