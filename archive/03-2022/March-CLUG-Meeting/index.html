March CLUG Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Mar 20, 2022

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at 143 Street Tacos, 80 S. Carmichael Ave., Sierra Vista, AZ on Saturday, 26 March 2022, from 1300-1500.  All are welcome so bring a family member, a friend, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux.  Street Tacos arguably serves the best tacos in Sierra Vista and possibly Southern Arizona so come early and enjoy some excellent food or eat during or after the meeting.  Please do not bring outside food or drink into the meeting.  If you have a Linux question or a problem be sure and raise it at the meeting and we will attempt to solve it for you before the end of the meeting.

Rex Bouwense
