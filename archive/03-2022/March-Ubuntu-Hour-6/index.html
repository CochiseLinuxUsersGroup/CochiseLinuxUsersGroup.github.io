Ron,

Print a report that will us.

a Press Menu.
b Press a or b to display Print Reports and then press Print Reports.
c Press a or b to display WLAN Report and then press WLAN Report.
d For MFC-J825DW/J835DW and DCP-J925DW
Press Black Start or Color Start.
Press Star

Post it.

The IP should be there.

Then using this IP open a terminal and ping

#ping 192.168.xxx.xxx and it will tell you if the printer is available
in your lan and if your OS does not block it (firewall?)
