Re: March Ubuntu Hour

From Teruel deCampo MD · Mar 16, 2022

Ron,

You said you had all the OS connected wireless to you printer and now
they do not work, correct? I would try to fix that instead of creating
a whole new "system"

1. Which Printer?

2. What type of wireless connection did you have?
1. LAN?
1.1 Fixed IP?
1.2 DHCP?
1.3 Can you ping the printer?
2. Direct Wireless?
1.1 Direct Wireless in ad hoc connection wireless between 2
devices. AFAIK Wireless Direct broadcast in a different subnet than the
one use the the printer is connected through the Ethernet (?)
1.2 Direct Wireless is enable by default. You can have both
enable at the same time or have one of the two off. Direct Wireless
requires a password!
3. Cloud Printing but this is a new bag of worms so most likely
it is not your case.

-=teruel=-

From Teruel deCampo MD · Mar 19, 2022

Ron,

Print a report that will us.

a Press Menu.
b Press a or b to display Print Reports and then press Print Reports.
c Press a or b to display WLAN Report and then press WLAN Report.
d For MFC-J825DW/J835DW and DCP-J925DW
Press Black Start or Color Start.
Press Star

Post it.

The IP should be there.

Then using this IP open a terminal and ping

#ping 192.168.xxx.xxx and it will tell you if the printer is available
in your lan and if your OS does not block it (firewall?)

-=teruel=-
