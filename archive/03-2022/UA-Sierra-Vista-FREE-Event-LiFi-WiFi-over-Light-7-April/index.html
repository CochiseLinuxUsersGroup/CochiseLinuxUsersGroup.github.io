It's been a while, but our free speaker series with IEEE is starting to come back on campus here in Sierra Vista. If you are able to share the attached/below with your network, I'd appreciate it. The topic is super interesting - I attended this talk in January and wanted to bring it to Sierra Vista. I hope you can attend too - it's been a while since I've seen some of you! John

On Thursday, 7 April at UA Sierra Vista there will be a free talk and demonstration (with lunch provided) from 11am-12pm on LiFi by Fernando De Los Reyes with Wildflower International. LiFi is the use of lightwave technologies for data transmission, or how light fixtures can become access points. RSVP is required for this technical talk sponsored by the IEEE Ft Huachuca Section, as seating is limited. The attached flyer has details. RSVP at http://ce.arizona.edu/LiFi
