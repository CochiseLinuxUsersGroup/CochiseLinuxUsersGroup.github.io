Cochise Linux User Group Monthly Meeting 28 January 2025

From Rex Bouwense · Jan 27, 2025

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at the Warrior Healing Center (main conference room), 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday 28 January, 1700-1800 (5-6:00pm).  I will be conducting a presentation on repairing GRUB from the grub> and grub rescue> prompts.

Bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them. 

Bring a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux and FOSS.
   
See you there.

Rex Bouwense
