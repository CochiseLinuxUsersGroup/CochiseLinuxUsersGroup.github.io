From Jeff Denton · Jul 28, 2015

I haven't lived in Arizona for a long time. After I left, I couldn't
find anyone interested in taking over the LUG.

I let the cochiselinux.org / .net / .com domains expire back in 2008.
Someone bought the domains later. They resurrected the website by
puling a copy from the WayBack Machine. I contacted the new owner and
asked them if this was a new form of cyber squatting. They said no,
they liked the old website. I asked them to add a new post to the
front page letting everyone know the LUG was no longer active. That
didn't happen.

I'm still the owner / administrator / moderator of the mailing list.
Again, only because I haven't found anyone interesting in taking over.

If someone wants to resurrect Coshiselinux, they will have to contact
the owner of the domains and ask them if they are willing to sell.
Look at the whois information to get contact information.

As for the mailing list, I can give ownership and administrative
access to whoever is interested.

Jeff
