Cochise Linux User Group Monthly Meeting 25 August 2026

From Rex Bouwense · Aug 23, 2026

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at the Warrior Healing Center's main conference room, 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday, 25 August, 1700-1800 (5-6 pm).  There will be a presentation on Desktop Environments.  Also, you are invited to bring your Linux questions and problems and hopefully an answer or solution can be provided.  As always you are asked to bring a family member, a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to the Linux users of the CLUG.

See you there.

Rex Bouwense
