Is it time to close our Discord server?

From Devi Garcia · Aug 13, 2026

Hi everyone,

I hope you’re all doing well! I wanted to touch base about our Discord server, as it’s been pretty quiet lately. It seems like activity has slowed down significantly, and I’m wondering if everyone is still interested in keeping it open.

I completely understand that life gets busy, and sometimes communities naturally wind down. If most of us feel it’s time to close the server, we can archive any important info and say our goodbyes. On the other hand, if there’s still interest in keeping it alive, maybe we can brainstorm ways to revive things!

No pressure at all—just wanted to check in and see what everyone thinks. Feel free to reply here or drop a quick message on the server if you have thoughts.

Thanks, and hope to hear from you soon!
