CLUG Monthly Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · May 20, 2023

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at the Warrior Healing Center (main conference room), 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday, 23 May, 1700-1830 (5-6:30pm).
Bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them. 
Bring a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux and FOSS. 

Noah will be will be giving a presentation on The Command Line. 
See you there.
