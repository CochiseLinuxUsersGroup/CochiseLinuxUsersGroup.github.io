Installation Festival

From Rex Bouwense · May 18, 2023

Just a reminder. that the CLUG will conduct an Installfest this
Saturday, 20 May 2023, 1000-1400 (that is 10 am. to 2 pm.) in the main
conference room of the Warrior Healing Center, 1838 Paseo San Luis. In
addition to installing we can also provide solutions to Linux computer
problems. So, if you have a problem, bring your computer and hopefully
we can provide assistance. All are welcome so bring your neighbors,
co-workers, friends, and relatives. See you there.

I will be there at 0900 to set up. Any help will be appreciated.

Rex Bouwense
