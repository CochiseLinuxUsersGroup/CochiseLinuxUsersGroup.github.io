Sierra Vista Ubuntu Hour

From Rex Bouwense · Aug 09, 2021

    The Cochise Linux User Group will host a Ubuntu Hour on Thursday 12 August 2021 from 1800-1900 (6-7 pm) at Schlotzsky's, 3900 E. Fry Blvd. in Sierra Vista.  While primarily a social event, there is always discussion of Ubuntu, Linux, and Free and Open Source Software.  If you have a problem or a question we will attempt to solve it or provide an answer before the end of the meeting.  Everyone is welcome, so bring a family member, a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to the wonderful world of Linux operating systems while enjoying a great sandwich and and a cup of coffee or soda.

Rex Bouwense
