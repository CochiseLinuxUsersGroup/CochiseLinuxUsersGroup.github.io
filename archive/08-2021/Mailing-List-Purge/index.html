Mailing List Purge

From Rex Bouwense · Aug 18, 2021

    We are attempting to update and purge our mailing list.  If you wish to continue to receive emails from subscribers to the Cochise Linux mailing list, please respond to this email within 14 days of receipt indicating your desire.  Non-responding individuals and individuals who indicate that they wish to be unsubscribed will then be unsubscribed and purged from this list.
    This list is a subscription only list and only subscribers are able to post messages.  Non-subscribers must have their posts approved before they are posted to the membership eliminating SPAM posts.  Emails that you receive will be one of four subjects;  announcements of upcoming meetings or events, minutes or reports of events that have occurred, links to websites that a member has found useful and wants to share with the membership, or requests for assistance associated with a Linux operating system or Free and Open Source Software and/or responses to the requests.  The mailing list is generally not used for other reasons.
    Thank you for your time.

Rex Bouwense
