Sierra Vista Ubuntu Hour

From Rex Bouwense · May 08, 2019

There will be a Ubuntu Hour at Schlotzsky's, 3900 E. Fry Blvd in Sierra
Vista on Thursday 09 May from 1800-1900. It is a social hour for Ubuntu
and other Linux users to get together and enjoy a great sandwich and a
cup of coffee or soda. Attend if you can and bring a family member,
co-worker, or friend to introduce them to the wonderful world of Linux
operating systems for their personal computer and other Free and Open
Source Software.
Rex Bouwense

Sierra Vista Ubuntu Hour

From Rex Bouwense · Nov 07, 2021

    The Cochise Linux User Group will host a Ubuntu Hour on Thursday 11 November 2021 from 1800-1900 (6-7 pm) at Schlotzsky's, 3900 E. Fry Blvd. in Sierra Vista.  While primarily a social event, there is always discussion of Ubuntu, Linux, and Free and Open Source Software.  If you have a problem or a question we will attempt to solve it or provide an answer before the end of the meeting.  Everyone is welcome, so bring a family member, a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to the wonderful world of Linux operating systems while enjoying a great sandwich and and a cup of coffee or soda.
Rex Bouwense
