Monthly Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Sep 19, 2021

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at 143 Street Tacos, 80 S. Carmichael Ave., Sierra Vista AZ at 1300-1500, 25 September 2021.  All are welcome so bring a family member, a friend, or a co-worker to introduce them to Linux.  Street Tacos serves arguably the best tacos in Sierra Vista and possibly Southern Arizona so come early and enjoy some excellent food or eat during or after the meeting.  Please do not bring outside food or drink into the meeting.  If you have a Linux question or a problem be sure and raise it at the meeting and we will attempt to solve it for you before the end of the meeting.  There will be a presentation and discussion on what to do if you turn your computer on and all you get is the dreaded grub> prompt.  It obviously doesn't happen very often, but when it does and eventually all of will see that prompt, you will know what to do.

Rex Bouwense
