Wi-Fi problem

From Daniel Thomas · Sep 03, 2021

My Wi-Fi suddenly quit, apparently it is not sensing any signals in the
area. My other laptop sitting next to it works fine. lshw shows
'Wireless Interface Network' Disabled. Network Connections shows the
modem used 2-days before (the last time I used it) & has the proper
setup. Looking at the panel obtained by clicking on the wifi button at
bottom right initially shows 'Enable Networking' checked but not the
'Enable Wi-Fi' box. Checking the latter does not change any of the
above. Leaving it checked & re-booting does not change anything -- it
comes back as unchecked. This is on my HP Pavilion Notebook running
Linux Mint 19.3 (fully updated). I tried using Timeshift to step back 1
week to when the Wi-Fi was working -- no change. I suppose it's a
hardware problem. Hopefully someone will have a different idea.

Re: Wi-Fi problem

From Snazzy Cipher · Sep 03, 2021

Hello,

Given that you have gone through your settings: 

To troubleshoot, I would use an external wifi adapter plugged into a usb port. That will let you know if the onboard adapter bit the dust pretty quick.

I keep one laying around the house for just this reason. AND older computers great for running linux can have wifi cards that support old B and G which isn't backwards compatible from AC which is what newer wifi routers run. 

Hopefully that helps.
Annette
