CLUG Installfest

From Rex Bouwense · Oct 31, 2018

For those of you that may have failed to place this event on your
calendar, the Cochise Linux User Group will be conducting an Installfest
on Saturday, 03 November 2018 from 1100-1500 at the Sierra Vista Public
Library, Mona Bishop room, 2600 E. Tacoma St., Sierra Vista. Please
attend if you can even if it is merely to stop by to show your support
for those of us that are participating in the event. Your support is
essential to the success of this event. Share this information with your
friends, co-workers, and neighbors who may not be aware that there is an
alternative to Microsoft Windows or Apple OS for their personal computers.

Rex Bouwense
