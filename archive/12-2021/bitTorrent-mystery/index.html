Re: bitTorrent mystery

From Rex Bouwense · Dec 21, 2021

George
You can decrease the time a torrent is active by changing a few items in preferences.  Open Transmission and select Edit from the horizontal menu.  A new page will open.  Then select Seeding.  Change the value of Stop seeding at ratio to 0.05.  Also change Stop seeding if idle for N minutes to 1.  That will give you a shorter time.

Rex
