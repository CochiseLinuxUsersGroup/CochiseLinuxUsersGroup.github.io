One of the reasons that I have emphasized that it is a very good idea to have a bootable flash drive with your current operating system on it (at least a half dozen times) is you can use it to boot to a live system and then install the boot repair software on your hard drive.  From there you can repair your system without having to reinstall the entire system thereby saving you the time and effort required to do so.  Excellent idea George we will discuss the PPAs at our January CLUG meeting on the 22nd.
