Mini Linux Workshops

We have decided since at least since one of us is always at the Warrior Healing Center every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday between 9am to 12pm we would turn these into "Mini Linux Workshops" for any members to come and bring problems they have in to be fixed.. Hopefully We'll See You There..
