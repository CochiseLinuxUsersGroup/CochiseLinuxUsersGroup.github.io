Cochise Linux User Group Monthly Meeting 25 November 2025

From Rex Bouwense · Nov 23, 2025

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at Warrior Healing Center main conference room, 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday, 25 November, 1700-1830 (5-6:30pm).  George Self will be giving a presentation on Artificial Intelligence.  Bring a family member, a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to the Linux users of the CLUG.  Whenever we meet, conversations about Linux and FOSS always arise so bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them after the presentation.

See you there.

Rex Bouwense
