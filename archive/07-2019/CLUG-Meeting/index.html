CLUG Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Jul 23, 2019

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct a group meeting on 27 July
1300-1545 at the Sierra Vista Public Library conference room. There
will be a presentation on Working with GitHub by David Garcia and Rex
Bouwense. There are two installs scheduled plus any questions or
problems that the attendees happen to bring up. Attend if you can and
bring along a family member, friend, or co-worker to introduce him/her
to the wonderful world of Linux and Free and Open Source Software.
