Computer Repair and Install Workshop

From Rex Bouwense · Jul 27, 2019

Another computer repair workshop has been scheduled for Saturday,
03 August 2019, 1000-1400 at the Veteran Synergy Center, 1838 Paseo San
Luis, Sierra Vista. We will be meeting in the conference room (room
#30). Bring your spare parts, computers to be donated, and any computer
tools that you think you might need. Install media will be provided. A
map of the building is attached. For those of you who do not have access
to a GPS, the Veteran Synergy Center can be reached by driving South on
AZ Hwy 92 and turning left on La Linda Way (the street immediately after
Lawley Dodge). Continue on La Linda Way until it ends at Paseo San
Luis. Turn right and drive less than half a block. The building, 1838,
is on your right. Enter through the front door, turn right into the
hallway and then turn left at the very next hallway. The conference
room is located at the end of that hallway. See the map at the
attachment. Plan on attending if you can.
Rex Bouwense
