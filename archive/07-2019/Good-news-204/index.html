Good news

From Rex Bouwense · Jul 16, 2019

I have some good news and some better news. First, the good news. One
of our members, Lynn, discovered an office building that houses various
not for profit organizations that support our veterans at 1834 Paseo San
Luis in Sierra Vista. I talked with the owner and told him briefly what
we do and what we have done. He would be happy to have us participate.
There is a conference room available that will hold 30 plus people. The
conference room has a very large wall mounted TV that can be used for
presentations. It would be ideal for our monthly meetings,
installfests, computer repair workshops, Linux presenations, and just
about anything else we wanted to use it for. In addition they have a
computer lab which we could take over should we desire. They are
willing to accept computers with a Linux operating system installed on
them to be set up in the computer lab and he is sure that some of the
veterans would like computers with Linux. The owner himself is a Linux
user. The better news is that all of this is free. As we are growing
and continue to do more things we will soon outgrow the library
conference room. This seems to be a viable next step. We will discuss
this option at our next meeting on Saturday 27 July 2019 at the SV
public library.
Rex Bouwense
