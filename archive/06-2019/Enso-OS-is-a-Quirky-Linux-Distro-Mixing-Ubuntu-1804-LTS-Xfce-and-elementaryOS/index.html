Enso OS is a Quirky Linux Distro Mixing Ubuntu 18.04 LTS, Xfce, and elementaryOS

From Devi Garcia · Jun 11, 2019

Have you heard of a Linux distribution called Enso OS? I’ll admit that
I hadn’t, not until someone pointed it out to me on Twitter.

But from what I’ve seen, Enso OS is a unique Linux distro amidst its
peers in that it’s mix of Ubuntu 18.04 LTS, Xfce, and elementary OS.

This unique, quirky combination gives the OS a clear, clean, and
distinct identity; this is not your regular me-too Ubuntu derivative —
far from it!
