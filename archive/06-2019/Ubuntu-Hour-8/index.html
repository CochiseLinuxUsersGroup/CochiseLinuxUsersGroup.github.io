Ubuntu Hour

From Rex Bouwense · Jun 11, 2019

There will be a Ubuntu Hour at Schlotzsky's, 3900 E. Fry Blvd, in Sierra
Vista at 1800 (6 p.m.) Bring a family member or a friend and introduce
them to a free option to Microsoft Windows for their personal computer
while you are enjoying a great sandwich and a cup of coffee or a soda.
Plus you can get to know your fellow members of the Cochise Linux User
Group a little better in a social atmosphere.
Rex Bouwense
