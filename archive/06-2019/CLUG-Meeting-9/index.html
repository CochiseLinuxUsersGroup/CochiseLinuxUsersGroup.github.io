CLUG Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Jun 20, 2019

The Cochise Linux user Group will hold a meeting on Saturday, 22 June
1300-1545, at the Cochise County Public Library, 2600 E. Tacoma Street,
Sierra Vista. Bring a family member or friend to introduce them to the
wonderful world of Linux. There will be a presentation on "Why should
you change to Linux" as well as one scheduled re-install. Attend if you
can.
Rex Bouwense
