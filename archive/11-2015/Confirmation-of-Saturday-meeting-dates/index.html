Confirmation of Saturday meeting dates

From MajB · Nov 21, 2015

We were very lucky at the public library lottery on Friday.
Starting in January, our second Cochise Linux User Group meeting of the
month will occur on the fourth Saturday of each month at the Sierra
Vista public library conference room (2600 E. Tacoma, Sierra Vista AZ)
from 1400-1545. The first meeting of each month will still be at the
University of Arizona Sierra Vista campus, room C165A North (1140 N.
Colombo, Sierra Vista AZ) from 1700-1800 on the second Wednesday of
each month.
In addition to these meetings we will also endeavour to support the
University of Arizona Continuing Education's series of lectures when the
new list is released.
