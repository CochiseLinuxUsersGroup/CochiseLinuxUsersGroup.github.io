Cochise Linux User Group meeting

From MajB · Nov 13, 2015

This is just a friendly reminder that there will be a Cochise Linux User
Group meeting at 5 pm. on 18 November 2015 at the University of Arizona
Sierra Vista campus, in room C165A North, 1140 Colombo Ave, Sierra Vista
AZ. There will be a free presentation co-sponsored by the University of
Arizona Continuing Education Division and the IEEE Fort Huachuca Section
on Understanding and Implementing Agile Scrum. The regular meeting will
commence immediately after the presentation. If you plan to attend the
presentation please PSVP to Dr John DeLalla at jd@arizona.edu.
In addition to the two CLUG meetings this month, there will also be
a Ubuntu Hour on 19 November from 6-7 pm. at Schlotzsky's Cafe, 3900
East Fry Blvd., Sierra Vista AZ 85635. If you use a Ubuntu or other
Linux Operating System and want to chat about it over a cup of coffee,
glass of soda, or a great sandwich, or just want to learn about Linux
please attend.
Rex
