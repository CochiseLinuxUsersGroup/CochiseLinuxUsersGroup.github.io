Cochise Linux User Group Monthly Meeting 22 July 2025

From Rex Bouwense · Jul 20, 2025

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at Denny's, 2397 E. Fry Blvd, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday 22 July, 1700-1800 (5-6:00pm).  There will be no presentation this month.  We will only be meeting for supper so bring your family, a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to the Linux users of the CLUG.  As usual you can also bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them over supper.
See you there.

Rex Bouwense
