Sierra Vista Ubuntu Hour

From Rex Bouwense · May 08, 2019

There will be a Ubuntu Hour at Schlotzsky's, 3900 E. Fry Blvd in Sierra
Vista on Thursday 09 May from 1800-1900. It is a social hour for Ubuntu
and other Linux users to get together and enjoy a great sandwich and a
cup of coffee or soda. Attend if you can and bring a family member,
co-worker, or friend to introduce them to the wonderful world of Linux
operating systems for their personal computer and other Free and Open
Source Software.
Rex Bouwense

Sierra Vista Ubuntu Hour

From Rex Bouwense · Feb 08, 2021

The Cochise Linux User Group will host a Ubuntu Hour at Schlotzsky's, 3900 E. Fry Blvd. on Thursday, 11 February 1800-1900 (6-7 pm.).  While primarily a social event, the conversation always turns to Ubuntu, Linux, and Free and Open Source Software.  If you have a question or a problem bring it to the event and we will attempt to answer or solve it.  Bring a family member, a neighbor, a friend, or a co-worker and introduce them to the wonderful world of Linux and while you are at it enjoy a a great sandwich and a cup of coffee or a soda and mingle with other Linux users from the county.
Rex Bouwense
