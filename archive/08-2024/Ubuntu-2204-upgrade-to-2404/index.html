Ubuntu 22.04 upgrade to 24.04

From Rex Bouwense · Aug 16, 2024

For those of you using Ubuntu or one its "flavors", the path to upgrade
has been pushed back by Canonical from the 15th to the 29th of August.
So if you were waiting to upgrade your computer to the newest version
which is not possible until the release of the first point release (i.e.
24.04.1), you will have to wait for two more weeks. This does not
prohibit you from downloading and installing the new version (24.04)
yourself. Alternatively, if you are content with 22.04 and it meets all
of your needs, retain it. It will still receive updates until April 2027.

Rex
