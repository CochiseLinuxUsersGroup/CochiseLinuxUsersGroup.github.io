The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct an Ubuntu Hour on Thursday 11 August at Schlotzsky's, 3900 E. Fry Blvd from 1800-1900.  While primarily a social hour to meet other Linux users, discussions about Linux operating systems, Free and Open Source Software, and of course Ubuntu always arise.  Member problems are discussed and solutions are offered.  Everyone is welcome so bring a family member, a neighbor, or a co-worker to introduce them to the world of Linux.  Enjoy a great sandwich and a cup of coffee while meeting other Linux users and perhaps having problems solved or questions answered.  See you there.
