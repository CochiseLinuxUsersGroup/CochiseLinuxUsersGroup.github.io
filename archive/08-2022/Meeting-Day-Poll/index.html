Meeting Day Poll

From Rex Bouwense · Aug 20, 2022

There has been some discussion about moving the CLUG monthly meeting to
a week day after work. Therefore, we are asking the members to
participate in a poll to express their opinion to provide input before
the decision is made. The options are Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, or
retain the Saturday meeting dates. Please respond within 14 days by
listing the 4 days in order of your preference. We are trying to get a
consensus so please list your first choice first, your second choice
second, your third choice third, and your fourth choice fourth as in the
example below.

1st choice _______

2nd choice_______

3rd choice_______

4th choice________

The information you provide will be used to determine if we should move
our monthly meeting and if we do which day is preferred. Thank you.

Rex Bouwense
