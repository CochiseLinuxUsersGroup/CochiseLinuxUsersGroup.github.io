Re: GPG error

From Rex Bouwense · Aug 06, 2022

After a bit of research, you should be able to complete your upgrade after unchecking the offending repositories in "Software and Updates".  See https://cloudcone.com/docs/article/how-to-fix-the-repository-is-not-signed-error-on-ubuntu-20-04/  and https://askubuntu.com/questions/732985/force-update-from-unsigned-repository for detailed instuctions.  Apparently the PPA that you are using to update Google Earth is no longer supported.  By unchecking it, you should no longer get that message. 

Rex Bouwense
