There has been some discussion about moving the CLUG monthly meeting to a week day after work. Therefore, we are asking the members to participate in a poll to express their opinion to provide input before the decision is made. The options are Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, or retain the Saturday meeting dates. Please respond within 14 days by listing the 4 days in order of your preference. We are trying to get a consensus so please list your first choice first, your second choice second, your third choice third, and your fourth choice fourth as in the example below.

2nd choice__Tues_____
