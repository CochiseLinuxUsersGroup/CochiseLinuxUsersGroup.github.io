I am using an Ubuntu 22.04 "flavor" as my day to day operating system.  As you may or may not know Canonical has decided to install Firefox as a Snap program by default.  It is taking a bit longer to launch but I live with that.  However, every time there is an update to the program, I get a warning message that Snap will update in X days.  The message is annoying because it shows up daily.  I found out how to get rid of it.  You can either close Firefox and wait for Snap to update Firefox (which could take as long as 6 hours until the next Snap update) or close Firefox, open a terminal and type snap refresh firefox   This will update Firefox and it only takes seconds depending upon your internet speed.
