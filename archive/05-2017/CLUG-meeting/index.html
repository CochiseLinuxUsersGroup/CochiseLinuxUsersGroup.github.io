CLUG meeting

From MajB · Nov 20, 2015

A meeting of the Cochise Linux User Group, CLUG, was held on 18 November
2015 at the University of Arizona Sierra Vista campus, classroom C165A
North from 1700-1830. There was a presentation co-sponsored by the
University of Arizona Continuing Education Division and the IEEE Fort
Huachuca section on Understanding and Implementing Agile Scrum. There
were 17 attendees. The next meeting will be held on 5 December 2015 at
the Sierra Vista public library conference room at 1400-1545. After the
presentation, Q & A, and discussion, we will once again adjourn to
Vinny's for pizza and some great conversation that may or may not be
associated with Linux or FOSS.
Rex Bouwense
