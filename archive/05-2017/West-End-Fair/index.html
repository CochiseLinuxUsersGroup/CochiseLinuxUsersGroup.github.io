West End Fair

From Rex Bouwense · May 04, 2017

As some of you already know, the Cochise Linux User Group will be
manning an information booth at the West End Fair this Saturday, 6 May,
1000-1700, on Fry Blvd in Sierra Vista. If you have not committed to
volunteer your time to man the booth, please stop by and say hi to those
of us who will be passing out information about the CLUG and the Ubuntu
Arizona Local Community. Look for our banner. Thank you.

Rex Bouwense
