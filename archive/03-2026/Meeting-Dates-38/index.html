Meeting Dates

From Rex Bouwense · Mar 28, 2026

We have been experiencing lower than average attendance at our monthly
meetings, so once again I ask you to rank the days of the week from best
to worst for you to attend the meetings. An example would be:

"Tuesday, Saturday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Monday, Sunday. "

Please include all seven days. Also you can suggest a time if 5 pm is
not a good time for you. We really need your input. I am asking you to
respond no later than 30 April so your selections can be tabulated and a
decision made on whether we should move the dates of the future meetings.

Rex
