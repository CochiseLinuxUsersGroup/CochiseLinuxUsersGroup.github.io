CLUG Monthly Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Nov 17, 2022

The Cochise Linux User Group will hold its monthly meeting at the Warrior Healing Center (main conference room), 1838 Paseo San Luis, 1700-1830 (5-6:30 p.m.) this Tuesday 22 November.  Bring your computer problems and questions and we will attempt to solve them.  There will be a presentation by Tommy Worthey on Digitizing your videos and DVDs.  Bring a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux and FOSS.  See you there.

Rex Bouwense
