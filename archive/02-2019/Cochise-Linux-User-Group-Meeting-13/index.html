Cochise Linux User Group Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Feb 18, 2019

There is a meeting of the Cochise Linux User Group on Saturday 23
February 1300-1545 at the Sierra Vista Public Library conference room,
2600 E. Tacoma St.
There will be a presentation on Email and Thunderbird by Rex Bouwense.
We will also be discussing preparations for the next installfest in May,
our possible involvement in the University of Arizona South Family Day
in April, and perhaps a few other Linux related (and non-related
subjects). Please attend if you can. Your input is valuable so let us
know what you think. The traditional after meeting meal will take place
at 143 Street Tacos on Wilcox Dr.
Rex Bouwense
