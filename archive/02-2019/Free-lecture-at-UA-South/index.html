Free lecture at UA South

From Rex Bouwense · Feb 10, 2019

On Tuesday 26 February 2019 at 5:00-6:30 p.m. there will be a free
lecture at the University of Arizona South. It will take place in Groth
Hall in the Public Meeting Room. The topic will be "The Front Lines of
Networking in the Federal Government". "Learn how to move projects
forward and future-proof your career. Hear a perspective of what is
going on in the front lines of layers 1-3 in the Federal Government from
a vendor rep that works in the Federal sector. Examples will be used to
help show how to best move cyber projects forward and leverage synergies
to help advance the overall mission of operations. Hear about future
directions of networking tech and ways to future-proof your career so
you can continue to be an asset to any enterprise. It is a dynamic hour
covering the networking landscape as it looks and where it could go in
the next few years." The speaker will be Fernando De Los Reyes, the
director of Federal Sales for Ekahau, currently the #1 Software for
Active and Passive Wireless Site Surveys.
Pizza will be served
Please RSVP to Dr. John DeLalla at jd@arizona.edu

Rex Bouwense
