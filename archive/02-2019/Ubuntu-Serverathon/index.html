From Rex Bouwense · Feb 18, 2019

Excellent suggestion. Are you volunteering to give the presentation or
just asking if it can be done. AZLOCO which is a state-wide
organization with over 300 members in support of Ubuntu has been
attempting to organize a server class for individuals for over three
years but has run up against the problem of finding someone with the
expertise who will devote the time to preparing it and actually
conducting the class or classes. Since we are barely one tenth the size
with obviously fewer contacts we have the same problem. If you are
volunteering I will pencil you in at a future meeting of your choosing.
If you are aware of a "professional" who is willing to do this without
remuneration, please give me his contact information. Thank you.
Rex Bouwense

Re: Ubuntu Server-a-thon??

From Rex Bouwense · Feb 19, 2019

OK.  I will put the topic down on the want list for presentations and if we find someone who is willing and qualified to give such a presentation, we will schedule it.  As for AZLOCO it is Ubuntu-Arizona Local Community Team.  It too is composed of volunteers throughout the state and is committed to Ubuntu advocacy and education.  Most of the active members are from the Phoenix metropolitan area although we have 10 from the Sierra Vista area.  Website:  http://azloco.org/ and wiki-page:  https://wiki.ubuntu.com/ArizonaTeam.
Our presentations or at least the slides from them can be found on our website:  https://cochiselinuxusergroup.org/.  They have mainly been given by members of the group when there has been some interest in the subject.  FYI the next one is on Email and the Thunderbird email client this Saturday.
Rex Bouwense
