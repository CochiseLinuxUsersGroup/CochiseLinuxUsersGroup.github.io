Cochise Linus User Group Installfest

From Rex Bouwense · Apr 07, 2023

Our next Installation Festival is scheduled for Saturday 20 May
2023 in the main conference room of the Warrior Healing Center, 1838
Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista from 1000-1400. Attached is a flyer that
will be posted in various locations in and around town. I urge you to
print it and post it at your work, place of worship, and other locations
that you frequent. Remember one of our goals is to spread the use of
Linux throughout the county.

In addition to our normal presentation at our April meeting on the
25th, we will hold a review (for those of you who are interested or feel
that you need a refresher) of the post installation procedures.

Rex Bouwense
