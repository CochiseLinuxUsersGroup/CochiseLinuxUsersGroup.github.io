Ubuntu Hour

From Rex Bouwense · Aug 04, 2019

There will be a Ubuntu Hour on 08 August 1800-1900 at Schlotzsky's, 3900
E. Fry Blvd, Sierra Vista. Bring a friend, relative, or co-worker and
introduce them to a new operating system for their personal computer
while socializing with other Linux users and enjoying a great sandwich
and a cup or coffee or soda. Among other things we will be discussing
the results of the Computer Repair Install Workshop held on 03 August.
Rex Bouwense
