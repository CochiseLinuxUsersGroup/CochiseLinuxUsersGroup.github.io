CLUG Installfest

From Rex Bouwense · Oct 28, 2019

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct an Installation Festival on 02
November from 1000-1400 at the Warrior healing Center main conference
room. Bring a friend, family member, or co-worker and introduce them to
"The Wonderful World of Linux". Please attend if you can, even if it is
only to stop by to say hi, to support those of us who are assisting in
the effort. In addition to installing, we will also work on member
computer problems (time and expertise permitting) so if you have a
problem that you have not been able to figure out, bring your computer
and perhaps the group will be able to solve it for you or as a minimum
put you on the right track.

Rex Bouwense
