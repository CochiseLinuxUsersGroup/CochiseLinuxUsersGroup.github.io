Installfest 02 November

From Rex Bouwense · Oct 08, 2019

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct an Installation Festival on 02
November at 1000-1400 in the main conference room of the Warrior Healing
Center, 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, AZ. I would encourage you
all to attend and to bring a family member, a friend, or a co-worker to
introduce them to Linux and Free and Open Source Software. As a minimum
stop by and show your support for those of us who are participating. I
have attached a flyer for the event. Feel free to copy it and
distribute it to your family, friends, work place, and places of worship.
Rex Bouwense
