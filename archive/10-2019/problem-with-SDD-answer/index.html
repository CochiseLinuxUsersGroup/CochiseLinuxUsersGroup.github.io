problem with SDD - answer

From Eugene Zastera · Oct 18, 2019

At the last meeting I mentioned that I had an Intel 80G SDD  that shows only 8M available space.

 

Intel identifies it as a known problem with the firmware. 

 

Unfortunately, Intel will NOT give me the firmware upgrade to fix it, because I didn’t buy the drive from them.  It was embedded in a system, and for that reason they won’t let me download and install the firmware fix (which is clearly posted on their website).  Intel says go to the OEM who put the SSD in the computer and get an update from them.  That might take months to identify who even built the system.

 

Really great to know how Intel will treat those who have their products! 

 

So, there is the answer.  Bad firmware, and Intel won’t help.

 

Gene
