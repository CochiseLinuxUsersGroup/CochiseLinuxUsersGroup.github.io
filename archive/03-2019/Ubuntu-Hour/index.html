Ubuntu Hour

From MajB · Sep 25, 2015

A Ubuntu Hour was held on 24 September 2015 at Schlotzsky's Cafe on Fry
Blvd from 6-9 pm. Eventually the discussion moved to the Cochise Linux
User Group since many present are also members of the CLUG. Many of
those present expressed the desire for a second monthly CLUG meeting on
a Saturday to accommodate some people who have classes during the week,
work later, or are just unable to regularly attend a weekday meeting. I
am open to the idea but felt the need to bring this request to the
entire membership for comments. We will of course discuss this at the
next regular meeting which is still scheduled for 21 Oct. Please submit
any comments that you have or bring them with you and present them at
the next meeting.
A friendly reminder, there is a University of Arizona Continuing
Education free lecture on The Internet of Things on 6 October from 5-7
pm. in room C165A North. Please attend if you can.
Rex Bouwense

Ubuntu Hour

From Rex Bouwense · Mar 11, 2019

There is a Ubuntu Hour on Thursday, 14 March 2019, 1800-1900, at
Schlotzsky's, 3900 E. Fry Blvd. Sierra Vista AZ. While this is listed
as a Social Hour, where the Ubuntu Operating System is discussed over a
sandwich and a cup of coffee or a glass of soda, the conversation has
been known to spread to other Linux and Free and Open Source Software
topics and is certainly not limited to any one subject. Come and enjoy
meeting with other Linux users. We have solved Linux computer problems
and even conducted an installation during past Ubuntu Hours. Bring
friends and introduce them to Linux and FOSS.

Rex Bouwense
