Installfest 04 May 2019

From Rex Bouwense · Mar 18, 2019

The Cochise Linux User Group in conjunction with the Ubuntu Arizona
Local Community Team will be conducting an Installation Festival on 04
May 2019, 1100-1500 at Livia's Coffee, 2480 E. Wilcox Dr. in Sierra
Vista. I have attached a flyer for you to duplicate and share with your
family, friends, co-workers, and places of worship. Please do so. I
would also encourage you to participate in the event even if it is to
stop by and say hello to the members who have decided to devote some of
their free time to expand the use of Linux operating systems and Free
and Open Source Software in Cochise County. However, should you decide
to assist with the installing please contact me and we will schedule a
time for you to help.
Rex Bouwense
