Depending on the importance of data, an offsite backup is a great idea.
One thing to think about as far as external drives go, is sometimes the
drive is fine but the housing is what has failed. I have had that
happen several times especially with the larger enclosures, sometimes it
is the USB to SATA interface that fails or the power shorts housing.
These types of failures are NOT common though, so external drives are
still a viable option in my opinion.

Thanks,
Robert

From Rex Bouwense · Mar 15, 2019

I was out most of the day, but some of the comments that have been offered are great.  A cloud is an excellent choice for a back-up to the back-up and so is an external hard drive.  The chances of three hard drives failing all at once are so tiny.  I back my stuff up on flash drives and SD cards and have been doing so since I was using Microsoft Windows 98.  I have had only one hard drive die on me and no flash drives or SD cards.  It is a matter of choice and you have a plethora of options out there.
Rex
