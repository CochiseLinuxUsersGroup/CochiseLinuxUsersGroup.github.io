Cochise Linux User Group Meeting 23 March 2019

From Rex Bouwense · Mar 19, 2019

There is a meeting of the Cochise Linux User Group at the Sierra Vista
Public Library conference room, 2600 E. Tacoma St. on 23 March 2019 at
1300. There will be a presentation on the History of Unix, BSB, and
Linux. Attend if you can. We will also be discussing preparations for
the upcoming Installfest on 04 May 2019 at Livia's Coffee, 2480 E.
Wilcox Dr.
Rex Bouwense
