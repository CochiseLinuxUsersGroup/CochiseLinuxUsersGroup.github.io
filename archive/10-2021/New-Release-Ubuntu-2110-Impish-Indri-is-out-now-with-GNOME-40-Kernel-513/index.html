[New Release] Ubuntu 21.10 &#x27;Impish Indri&#x27; is out now with GNOME 40, Kernel 5.13

From Devi Garcia · Oct 14, 2021

What's new with Ubuntu 21.10? A few quick items:

Quoted reply (15 lines)

Linux Kernel 5.13 bringing new hardware
support
  • Linux Kernel 5.13 bringing new hardware support
  • Firefox 93, with it now a Snap package as a joint effort between Canonical and Mozilla
  • Wayland session available for NVIDIA GPU users
  • PulseAudio 15 bringing support for Bluetooth LDAC and AptX codecs, as well as HFP Bluetooth profiles providing better audio quality
  • GNOME 40
  • LibreOffice 7.2.1
  • Thunderbird 91.1.2

GNOME 40 is probably the biggest user-facing change, as that release came with the redesigned Activities Overview. More on the differences in GNOME 40 in our previous article. However, Ubuntu continues to add in their dock on the left so it's not a stock GNOME 40 experience making it a bit easier to use.
