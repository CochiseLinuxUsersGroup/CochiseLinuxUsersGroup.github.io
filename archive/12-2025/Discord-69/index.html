Discord.

From Devi Garcia · Dec 16, 2025

I know we always mention this in our meetings, and a couple of you have joined and some may not even be sure what it is... Well it's basically a communication server/chatroom we created that once you make a discord.com account you can join and talk through... It supports private messages to other users/joining servers of whatever community you'd like to join and participate in...  It supports Text, Voice and Video communication...

It's much more versatile than the mailing list here.. we have channels/rooms specifically for just chatting, sharing screenshots, for gaming or even linux applications and scripts you may want to know about... there is also a channel dedicated to linux video reviews/tutorials i post so i don't flood the mailing list...

Today i just converted "Troubleshooting" "QnA" and "Requests" of our channels to a forum format so when once asks/posts a question or request it will get its own thread of comments from other server members so we won't lose the answers or be completely disorganized..

Joining the server linked below is probably in best foot forward for anyone wanting quick and easily found answers of questions.. or any requests of things you'd like to see the CLUG do or Present on...

No one likes change.. but this one could benefit everyone if we get a lot more activity over there..  It also has event notifications and reminders for anyone always forgetting the dates of those as well..

Hopefully we'll see you over there soon.


Discord Join Link:
https://discord.gg/ym2hCH5FSk
