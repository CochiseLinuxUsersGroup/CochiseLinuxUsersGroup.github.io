Ventoy

From Rex Bouwense · Dec 20, 2025

At our next meeting (23 December) at the Warrior Healing Center we will
have a computer set up to exclusively create Ventoy flash drives. If
you have not created one already and want to have one for yourself you
must bring your own empty flash drive (because all data on it will be
over written). For those of you that do not know how to do this, are a
little unsure of the procedure, or are a bit put off by using the
terminal, there will be someone there who can provide assistance.
Remember that you can put multiple ISOs on the flash drive and can store
additional files on the the same drive without losing the capability of
booting from any of the ISOs that have been written to it so the larger
the better.

Rex
