Cochise Linux User Group Monthly Meeting 23 December 2025

From Rex Bouwense · Dec 21, 2025

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at Warrior Healing Center main conference room, 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday, 23 December, 1700-1830 (5-6:30pm).  This meeting will be in the form of a workshop.  There will be three computers set up ready to have Ubuntu 24.04 installed on them for any of our new members who wish to practice installing on a computer.  They will then be listed as available for donation.  There will of course be experienced individuals on hand to provide assistance for these installs.  In addition, members are still invited to bring their Linux computer problems for assistance.  Bring a family member, a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to the Linux users of the CLUG.  Whenever we meet, conversations about Linux and FOSS always arise so bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them.

See you there.

Rex Bouwense
