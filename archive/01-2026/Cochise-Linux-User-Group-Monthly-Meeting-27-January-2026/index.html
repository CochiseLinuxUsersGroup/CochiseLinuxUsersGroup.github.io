Cochise Linux User Group Monthly Meeting 27 January 2026

From Rex Bouwense · Jan 25, 2026

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at Native Grill and Wings, 3950 Martin Luther King Jr Pkwy, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday 27 January, 1700-1830 (5-6:30pm).  There will be no presentation this month.  This will be a no host supper meeting, so bring your family, a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to the Linux users of the CLUG, while enjoying supper at one of our local restaurants.  Wherever and whenever we meet, conversations about Linux and FOSS always arise so bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them over supper. 

See you there.

Rex Bouwense
