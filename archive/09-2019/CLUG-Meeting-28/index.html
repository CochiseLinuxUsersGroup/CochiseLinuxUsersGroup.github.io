CLUG Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Sep 23, 2019

There is a meeting of the Cochise Linux User Group on Saturday, 28
September 1300-1545 at the Sierra Vista public library, 2600 E. Tacoma
Street. The presentation will be "Using Libre Office". We will be
discussing Libre Office Writer, Calc, and Impress. An actual document,
spreadsheet, and slide show will be created. Attend if you can.
In addition there will be an encore presentation of "Why you should
consider switching your computer operating system to Linux" on Thursday,
26 September 1730-1830 at the Warrior Healing Center, 1838 Paseo San
Luis. The target audience is of course veterans and their families.
Rex Bouwense
