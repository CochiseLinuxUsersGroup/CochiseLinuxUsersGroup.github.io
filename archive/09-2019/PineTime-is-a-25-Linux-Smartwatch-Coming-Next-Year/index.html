PineTime is a $25 Linux Smartwatch, Coming Next Year

From Devi Garcia · Sep 15, 2019

A $25 Linux Smartwatch is in development by Pine64, makers of the PineBook Pro Linux laptop. 

 

Tentative PineTime features include:

  • Heart rate monitor
  • Multi-day battery
  • Desktop charging dock
  • Zinc alloy & plastic chassis
  • Approx $25 price tag
