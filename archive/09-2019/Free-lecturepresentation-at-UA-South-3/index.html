As many of you already are aware, the University of Arizona South Continuing Education Department and the Fort Huachuca Section of the IEEE co-host free lectures each semester. The first one of the Fall semester is scheduled for 3 October 1700 - 1830 at Groth Hall (Public Meeting Room). Dolores H. Hill, a member of the OSIRIS-REx mission Communication and Public Engagement team, OSIRIS-REx Ambassadors lead, and co-lead of its Target Asteroids! Citizen Science program, will discuss NASA's OSIRIS-REx mission to near-Earth asteroid Bennu to provide additional information on our understanding of the origin and evolution of the early Solar System, organic materials important to life on Earth, and insight on how small asteroids migrate from the Main Asteroid belt to the inner Solar System. The spacecraft is expected to collect the asteroid sample next summer. The protected sample will return to Earth in September 2023. Currently, the science and flight dynamics teams are acquiring additional data and mapping details of the recently selected "Final Four" sample sites. Join the discussion about this exciting mission with a Senior Research Specialist at the Lunar and Planetary Laboratory at the University of Arizona. See the attached flyer for details. RSVPs are required since seating is limited and so sufficient food can be on hand for those attending.
RSVP to Dr. John DeLalla (jd@arizona.edu).

Rex Bouwense
