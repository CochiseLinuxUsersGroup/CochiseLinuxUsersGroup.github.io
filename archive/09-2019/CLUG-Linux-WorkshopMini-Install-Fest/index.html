CLUG Linux Workshop/Mini Install Fest

From Rex Bouwense · Sep 04, 2019

Reminder for those of you that have forgotten and seldom look at the
Group calendar, the Cochise Linux User Group will conduct a Computer
repair Workshop and mini-install fest on Saturday, 07 September from
1000 to 1400 at the Warrior Healing Center main conference room, 1838
Paseo San Luis. We are at a point where we can hopefully finish
renovating the laptop computers that have been donated and we are better
organized to complete this task. Many of the computers are almost
ready. Drop by even if you cannot stay and say hi to those who are
working to show your support for the Group.

Rex Bouwense
