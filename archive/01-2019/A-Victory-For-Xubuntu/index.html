A Victory For Xubuntu!

From Eliter Awesomeness · Jan 09, 2019

Hey guys,
 I had setup my grandfather with Xubuntu about two years ago, and he was optimistic at first, but he started to be convinced that his illegal copy of Windows from his random Hispanic friend (that probably screwed him and stole parts from him) was better than the copy of Xubuntu I put on his computer. He ONLY uses the web browser, so switching operating systems was not a loss. The problem was that he thought because he was using Windows he never has to sign in to Google (or "gmail" as he knew it as). As soon as I pressured him to sign in to Google in Chromium, he magically had no more problems with Xubuntu, and stopped restarting the computer to switch back to Windows each time he forgot.

Anyway, I'm happy for the win. He is no longer using an illegal copy of Windows, and he is now a part of the world of Free and Open Source Software. :)

-Eli D.
