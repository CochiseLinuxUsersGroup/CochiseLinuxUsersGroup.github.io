Ft. Huachuca FREE Talk Introduction to PNT and Mitigating Select GPS Vulnerabilities; 23 January @ UA Sierra Vista

From Rex Bouwense · Jan 14, 2019

There is another FREE presentation at the UA South on 23 January. If you
are interested in attending please rsvp to Dr John Delalla via email as
seating is limited. See the attachment.
"Details:
Introduction to PNT and Mitigating Select GPS Vulnerabilities Join us
for a lively hour long talk on the fascinating topics of Position,
Navigation, and Timing (PNT) with a brief review of Time & Frequency. We
will cover the PNT Basics and an introduction to Inertial Navigation and
Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs). Finally we will explore Global
Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS) and their Vulnerability - Causes and
Solutions, discussing some specific vulnerabilities, and tactics for
mitigation."

Rex Bouwense
