Amazon has an $86 Chrome computer the size of a flash drive – BGR

From Devi Garcia · Jan 16, 2019

If you thought it was impressive that you can buy a perfectly adequate Chromebook laptop computer for under $200, wait until you see this. The ASUS CHROMEBIT CS10 Stick-Desktop PC is a tiny dongle the size of a flash drive. Connect it to the HDMI port on any computer monitor or even a television, and you’ve instantly got yourself a full-fledged desktop computer running Google’s Chrome OS. It’s fantastic, and it’s only $86 on Amazon right now.
