[Link] The New Pinebook Pro Will Challenge Google Chromebooks For $199

From Devi Garcia · Jan 30, 2019

The Pinebook is a marvelous little Linux laptop, but at $99 it’s not exactly built to be your daily driver. It was created for tinkering and learning. That’s something Pine64 even admits. But the Pinebook has its ardent fans, and those fans have repeatedly requested a model that brings more RAM, more storage and better daily usability to the table. Today that request is being answered with the Pinebook Pro, a laptop boasting some admirable specs for its proposed $199 asking price.

Specs:
  • 14″ 1080p IPS LCD panel
  • 64GB of eMMC storage (registered forum members get 128GB)
  • 10000mAh battery
  • Black magnesium alloy body
  • SD card slot
  • Digital video output via USB-C
  • Audio aux out / UART
  • USB 3.0 and USB 2.0 ports
  • 802.11ac WiFi
  • Bluetooth 4.2
  • 4GB of LPDDR4 RAM
  • Charging via barrel port or USB-C
  • Rockchip RK3399 Hexacore SOC
  • 2mpx front-facing camera
  • Microphone
