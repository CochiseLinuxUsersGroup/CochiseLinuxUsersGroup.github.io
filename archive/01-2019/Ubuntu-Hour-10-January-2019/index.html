Ubuntu Hour 10 January 2019

From Rex Bouwense · Jan 06, 2019

Our first Ubuntu Hour of 2019 will take place at Schlotzsky's on 10
January from 1800-1900. Attend if you can. Remember this is a social
hour for people to mix with other Ubuntu and other Linux operating
system users to talk about Linux and Free and Open Source Software. It
is also a time to raise problems or questions that you may have. Bring
a friend and introduce them to Linux and FOSS while you enjoy a great
sandwich and a cup of coffee or a soda.
Rex Bouwense
