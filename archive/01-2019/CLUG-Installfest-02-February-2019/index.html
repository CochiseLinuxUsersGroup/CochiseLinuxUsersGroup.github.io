CLUG Installfest 02 February 2019

From Rex Bouwense · Jan 02, 2019

The first CLUG Installfest of 2019 will take place on 02 February from
1100-1500 at Livia's Coffee, 2480 E. Wilcox Dr. This will be the first
time that we will be holding an event there. Of course coffee,
non-coffee, frozen drinks, and specialty drinks will be available to
purchase as well as a limited supply of pastries. The room is smaller
than the rooms where we have previously been conducting our installfests
but it is more than sufficient to meet our needs. Attached is a flyer
which I encourage you to print out and post at your job site and place
of worship. Please share the information contained on it with your
family, friends, neighbors, and co-workers. The flyer will be posted at
locations in and around Sierra Vista and Fort Huachuca during the first
week of January. Because there is only about a month before the
Installfest, if you are interested in assisting please contact me as
soon as possible with the times that you will be available to work.
Whether you decide to assist or not please stop by the event to show
your support for those who are actually volunteering their time and
knowledge to further spread the use of Linux operating systems and free
and open source software throughout Cochise County. Thank you.
Rex Bouwense
