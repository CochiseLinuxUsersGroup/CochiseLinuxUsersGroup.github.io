Upcoming Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Jan 22, 2019

The Cochise Linux User Group will hold its monthly meeting on Saturday
26 January from 1300-1545 at the Sierra Vista Public Library conference
room, 2600 E, Tacoma Street. In a continuing effort to find a "go to"
light and user friendly operating system to be installed on older under
powered computers, there will be presentations on Peppermint and MX
Linux. We will also be discussing preparations for the upcoming
Installfest on 02 February from 1100-1500 at Livia's Coffee, 2480 E.
Wilcox Drive and a proposed change to the mailing list. Attend if you can.

Rex Bouwense
