CLUG Installfest

From Rex Bouwense · Jan 23, 2017

***Reminder***
The CLUG/AZLOCO installfest will take place this Saturday, 28
January 2017 from 1000-1600 at the University of Arizona South, Sierra
Vista campus, 1140 N. Colombo Ave, Sierra Vista AZ in the Learning
Resource Center. If you have not been in contact with one of the Group
Leaders to volunteer for a time frame to assist the in the installation
feel free to show up anyway. Your knowledge and experience will always
be welcome.
In addition there is a Ubuntu Hour scheduled at Schlotzsky's Cafe,
3900 E. Fry Blvd. this Thursday, the 26th of January 2017 from
1800-1900. Attend if you can and enjoy a cup of coffee or a glass of
soda and some interesting conversation about Ubuntu, other Linux
operating systems, and Free and Open Source Software.
Rex Bouwense
