The CLUG will conduct its monthly meeting on Saturday 22 August, from
1300-1545, at the Warrior Healing Center, 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra
Vista.  We will be meeting in their main conference room. Since the
front door will be locked, you must enter from the rear door.  You can
of course park in the front parking lot and then proceed to the rear of
the building (West side) and enter through the gate and the rear door
which will be open.  Follow the CLUG signs and arrows to the main
conference room (which will only require two right turns).  There will
be a presentation on "What to do if Ubuntu Will Not Boot" as well as our
first mini-presentation. We will also hold a practice installation for
anyone who believes that they need some additional training on
installing and the steps that should be completed immediately after the
install.  The Put & Take box will be present.  We will of course
continue to solve Linux problems and answer any questions raised by
members and/or guests.
