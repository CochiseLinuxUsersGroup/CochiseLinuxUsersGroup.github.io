[article/news] The Next PinePhone Community Edition Has Been Announced

From Devi Garcia · Aug 31, 2020



Priced from $149, the PinePhone Manjaro Community Edition also features a branded back cover bearing the Manjaro logo and the slogan “enjoy the simplicity”. This is exclusive to the community edition model (at least for now).

Pine64 will donate $10 from every unit sold to the Manjaro development team.

But when can you buy it? Soon.

You can preorder the Manjaro PinePhone Community Edition from mid-September direct from the Pine64 web store. It is available in 2GB RAM/16GB eMMC and 3GB/32GB eMMC configurations. The latter of these costs $199 but comes with a (very handy) USB Type-C dock accessory to support external devices, ethernet, and more.
