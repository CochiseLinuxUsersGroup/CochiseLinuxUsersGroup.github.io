Run your own privacy-first ad blocking service within the Free Usage Tier on Google Cloud.



This guide gets you set up with a Google Cloud account, and walks you through setting up a full tunnel (all traffic) or split tunnel (DNS traffic only) VPN connection on your Android & iOS devices, and computers.

The goal of this guide is to enable you to safely and privately use the Internet on your phones, tablets, and computers with a self-run VPN Server in the cloud.

Benefits:

  1. It can be run at no cost to you.

  2. Shields you from intrusive advertisements

  3. Speeds up connections by not needing to download ad resources.

  4. Blocks your ISP/cell phone company/public WiFi hotspot provider, and apps/websites from gaining insight into your usage activity.
