The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct a Ubuntu Hour at Schlotzsky's,
3900 E. Fry Blvd. on this Thursday, 13 August, from 1800-1900.  While
primarily a social hour to meet other Linux users in a very relaxed
atmosphere, there is always the inevitable Linux question or problem
raised that is almost always answered within the hour.  If you are in
need of a cable or another part for your computer check out our Put &
Take box which will of course be present.  Join us for a great sandwich,
a cup of coffee or soda, and an enjoyable time.  Better yet bring a
family member, a friend, a neighbor, a co-worker and introduce them to
Linux and Free and Open Source Software.
