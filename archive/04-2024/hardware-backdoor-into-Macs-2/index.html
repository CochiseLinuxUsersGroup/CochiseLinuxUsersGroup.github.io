Re: hardware backdoor into Macs

From Rex Bouwense · Apr 06, 2024

After doing a bit of research I discovered that the bug only affects M1,
M2, and M3 chip sets (from the original video) and is a local bug which
means that the attacker must have physical access to the computer.
Those of you that are using older Apple products need not be concerned
because the the M1 chipset was introduced in 2020, the M2 chipset in
June of 2022, and the M3 chipset in October of 2023. If your Apple
hardware is older than 2020 then you do not have any of those unless a
newer CPU has been installed.

Rex
