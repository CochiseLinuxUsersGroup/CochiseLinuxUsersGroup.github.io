Installfest 18 May 2024

From Rex Bouwense · Apr 06, 2024

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct an Installation Festival
at the Warrior Healing center main conference room on 18 May 2024 from
1100-1500 (11 am to 3 pm). I am attaching a flyer for you to print out
to share with your family, friends, and neighbors. Please also post a
copy (if allowed) at your workplace and place of worship. Thank you.

Our primary mission will be to install the new Ubuntu version
(24.04) on CLUG computers that are currently stored at the Warrior
Healing Center. (There are currently seven of them.) We of course will
also conduct installations on any additional computers that are brought in.

Rex
