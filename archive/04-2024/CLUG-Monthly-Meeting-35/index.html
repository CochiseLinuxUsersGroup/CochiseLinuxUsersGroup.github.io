CLUG Monthly Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Apr 20, 2024

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at the Warrior Healing Center (main conference room), 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday 23 April, 1700-1800 (5-6:00pm).  There will be a presentation on Open Suse (a Linux computer operating system) by Teruel (Terry) de Campo. 

Bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them. 
Bring a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux and FOSS.   
See you there.

Rex Bouwense
