Wow, Never had any problems with Thunderbird. Reference your junk
messages. There is an option in Settings->Privacy and Security that you
have to check so when you mark a message as junk it will go to the Junk
folder. Make sure that you have that checked. Not sure why you are
getting three copies of emails though.
