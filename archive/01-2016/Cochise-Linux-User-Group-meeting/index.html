Cochise Linux User Group meeting

From MajB · Oct 21, 2015

A CLUG meeting was held on 21 October 2015 at 5-6:30 pm. in classroom
C165A North, University of Arizona Sierra Vista campus. There was a
presentation on Engineering Careers in the Electrical Utility Industry
co-sponsored by the University of Arizona Continuing Education
Department and the IEEE Fort Huachuca Section. Those of you that
couldn't make it, we missed you. The next CLUG meeting is scheduled for
Saturday 7 November, 2-4 pm, at the Sierra Vista public library
conference room. Try to make it if you can. There is also a Ubuntu Hour
scheduled for tomorrow at the Schlotzsky's Cafe, 3900 East Fry Blvd. 6-7
pm. The purpose is to provide a forum to educate the public about
Ubuntu. If you use Ubuntu and would like to meet others who do as well
or just want to learn about it, plan to join us for some lively
conversation.
Rex Bouwense

Cochise Linux User Group meeting

From MajB · Jan 11, 2016

Just a reminder that there will be a Cochise Linux User Group
meeting on 13 January 1700-1800 at the University of Arizona Sierra
Vista campus room C165A North. There will be a presentation on GIT as a
primer for our next presentation on 23 January 1400-1545 at the Sierra
Vista public library conference room on creating free web sites with
Jekyll. Please attend if you can.
Other upcoming events are SCaLE x14 at the Pasadena Conference
Center 21-26 January. There are 150 expected exhibitors and 130
sessions, tutorials, and special events.
https://www.socallinuxexpo.org/scale/14x Full access pass is only $75
and a 50% discount code can be obtained. Contact me if you are
interested. Running simultaneously at the same location is UbuCon from
21-22 January. Mark Shuttleworth is scheduled to be the keynote
speaker.
https://insights.ubuntu.com/2015/10/21/announcing-the-ubucon-summit/
There will also be Ubuntu Hour on 28 January 1800-1900 at Scholtzsky's
Cafe on Fry Blvd. Once again attend if you can.
