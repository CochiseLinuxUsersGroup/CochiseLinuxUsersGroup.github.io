Cochise Linux User Group meeting

From MajB · Jan 20, 2016

There will be a meeting of the CLUG at the Sierra Vista public
library conference room, 2600 East Tacoma St. on 23 January 2016 at
1400-1545. After leaving the library we will reconvene at Vinny's for
some lively discussion on various Linux/Foss subjects (and perhaps some
unrelated) and some excellent pizza. Please attend if you can.
In addition there will be a Ubuntu Hour at Schlotzsky's Cafe on 28
January hosted by Rex Bouwense at 1800-1900. Please join us for some
Linux conversation and a great sandwich if you can.
Rex Bouwense
