There's a discussion topic that browser search results are being purposely limited in order to cause more searches with more advertisement exposure.

Kagi is a service addressing encumbrances placed on searches by ads, tracking,...
https://kagi.com/

Being signed into a YouTube account is causing an algorithm influence of videos being pushed into view. I've started using a YouTube down-loader to qualify my decision to watch a video. Currently using yt-dlp which is providing command line familiarity.
https://wiki.archlinux.org/title/Yt-dlp

Ordered a Brax3 phone to reduce stealth tracking and unknowingly sending information to the data collectors. This phone has a 3.5mm earphone jack and accepts a Micro SD card. I ordered a 1TB card to substitute as an external storage drive.
https://www.indiegogo.com/en/projects/braxtechnologies/brax3-the-most-privacy-friendly-smartphone

Xorg is present at a deeper level.
