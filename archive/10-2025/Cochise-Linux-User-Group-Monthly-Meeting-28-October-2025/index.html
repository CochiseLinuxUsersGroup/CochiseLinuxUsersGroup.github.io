Cochise Linux User Group Monthly Meeting 28 October 2025

From Rex Bouwense · Oct 26, 2025

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at La Casita Restaurant and Cantina, 465 E. Fry Blvd, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday 28 October, 1700-1830 (5-6:30pm).  There will be no presentation this month.  This will be a no host supper meeting, so bring your family, a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to the Linux users of the CLUG, while enjoying some of the local Mexican cuisine at one of Sierra Vista's oldest restaurants.  Wherever we meet, conversations about Linux and FOSS always arise so bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them over supper. 

See you there.

Rex Bouwense
