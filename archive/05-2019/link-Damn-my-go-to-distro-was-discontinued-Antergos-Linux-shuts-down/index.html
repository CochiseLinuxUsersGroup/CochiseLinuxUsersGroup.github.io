[link] Damn.. my go to distro was discontinued... Antergos Linux shuts down

From Devi Garcia · May 22, 2019

Beginner-friendly Arch Linux based distribution Antergos has announced
that the project is being discontinued.

Arch Linux has always been considered a no-go zone for the beginners.
Antergos challenged this status quo and made Arch Linux accessible to
everyone by providing easier installation method. People who wouldn’t
dare installing Arch Linux, opted for Antergos.
