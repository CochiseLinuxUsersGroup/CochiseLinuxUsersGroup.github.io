CLUG Installfest 0n 04 May 2019

From Rex Bouwense · May 01, 2019

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct an Installation Festival on 04
May 2019 at Livia's Coffee, 2480 E. Wilcox Drive in Sierra Vista from
1100 to 1500 hours. Attend if you can and please wear your CLUG
T-shirt. If you are unable to assist in the event, you could always
stop by and have a cup of coffee with those members who are working to
show your support. Bring a family member, a co-worker, or a friend and
introduce them to the wonderful world of Linux and Free and Open Source
Software. Remember we also do one on one discussions for those that are
interested in possibly switching their computer operating system from
Microsoft to Linux.

Rex Bouwense
