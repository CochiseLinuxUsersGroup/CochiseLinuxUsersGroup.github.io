[link] Purism’s New Offering is a Dream Come True for Privacy Concerned People

From Devi Garcia · May 01, 2019

You probably have heard about Purism. It’s a ‘Social Purpose
Corporation’ that has created the PureOS Linux distribution, Librem
series of laptops and Librem5 Linux-based smartphone. All of their
products are focused on protecting your privacy and giving you entire
control over your data.

Purism has increased its offering for privacy and security concerned
people with a new bundle of service, called Librem One.

https://itsfoss.com/librem-one/
