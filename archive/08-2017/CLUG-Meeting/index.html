CLUG Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Aug 07, 2017

Reminder
There is a Cochise Linux User Group meeting on Wednesday, 9 August 2017
at 5pm at the University of Arizona South, Sierra Vista campus, Learning
Resource Center. The address is 1140 N. Colombo Avenue, Sierra Vista
AZ. Among the items to be discussed will be the recent installation of
Ubuntu on a MacBook Pro and a Mac mini (will wonders never cease.) and
a simple way to reduce the swapiness on your Linux computer so it runs
even faster. Plus there will be some computer spare parts, to include
cables, cards, power supplies, and RAM chips, to be given away at no
cost on a first come first served basis. Please attend if you can.

Rex Bouwense
