From Rex Bouwense · Feb 05, 2023

I am by no means an expert on Apple products but I have conducted two Ubuntu installs on them.  One was a laptop and the second was a desktop.  In neither case was a conversion required.  After the ISO is downloaded and burned to a flash drive (Etcher is an excellent choice) merely boot from it.  As the computer is booting press the options key and choose the flash drive as the booting device.  The remainder of the install is the same as installing to any other computer.  At least that has been my experience and both installs that I have been involved with were successful.

Rex
