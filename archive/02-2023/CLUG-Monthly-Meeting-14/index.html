CLUG Monthly Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Feb 26, 2023

The Cochise Linux User Group will hold its monthly meeting at the Warrior Healing Center (main conference room), 1838 Paseo San Luis, 1700-1800 (5-6 p.m.) this Tuesday, 28 February.  Bring your computer problems and we will attempt to solve them.  Bring a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux and FOSS.  We will be talking about how to clean your Debian based OS and where to get support for your Ubuntu problems.  See you there.

Rex Bouwense
