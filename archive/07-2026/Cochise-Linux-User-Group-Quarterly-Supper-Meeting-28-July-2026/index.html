Cochise Linux User Group Quarterly Supper Meeting 28 July 2026

From Rex Bouwense · Jul 26, 2026

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its quarterly supper meeting at Denny's, 2397 E Fry Blvd, this Tuesday, 28 July, 1700-1800 (5-6 pm).  This is a quarterly supper meeting so bring a family member, a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to the Linux users of the CLUG without having too much nerdy stuff going on.  With the closing of Schlotzsky's, we will also be discussing where to move our monthly Ubuntu Hours so be there to provide your input.

See you there.

Rex Bouwense
