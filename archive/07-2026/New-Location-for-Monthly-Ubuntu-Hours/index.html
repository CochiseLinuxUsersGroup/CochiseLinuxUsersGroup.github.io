New Location for Monthly Ubuntu Hours

From Rex Bouwense · Jul 28, 2026

As you may or may not already know, Schlotzsky's will close its doors
for good on 12 August of this year which leaves us without a location
for our monthly Ubuntu Hours. For future Ubuntu Hours, we will now be
meeting at Carl's Jr., 2080 E. Fry Blvd. The dates (second Thursday of
each month) and the times (1800-1900 [6-7 pm]) will remain the same. We
had a very long and mutually beneficial relationship with the management
and workers at Schlotzsky's. I want to thank those of you who attended
the Ubuntu Hours in the past and hope that you will continue to attend
those in the future.

Rex Bouwense
