Re: Old kernels.

From Devi Garcia · Jul 04, 2026

The system of Ubuntu and Mint are auto set to keep old kernels in case of a newer one causing boot or HW issues so it's for a backup failsafe.. most people recommend to keep at least one older kernel if not  Two just in case but that's personal preferences of the newest works like you'd like can just keep that one and delete the others.. 

Running:

Sudo apt autoclean && sudo apt autoremove

On Ubuntu automatically removes unneeded depenancies and kerenls probably works for mint as well.
