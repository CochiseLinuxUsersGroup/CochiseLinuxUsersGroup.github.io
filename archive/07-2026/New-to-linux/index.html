From Devi Garcia · Jul 12, 2026

We generally do not offer windows support or recommend installing it alongside Linux because of Windows updates breaking the bootloader and causing issues similar to what your experiencing.. best solution I can give is either use two different drives for each OS or just use different computers per OS.. or pick your new main OS and use other in a VM of still actually needed ... Otherwise pretty much need to know exactly what happened when comes to windows or Linux whichever broke.. to be able to fix it.. and with a dual boot setup this will happen often.
