Cochise Linux User Group Monthly Meeting 27 May 2025

From Rex Bouwense · May 25, 2025

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at the Warrior Healing Center (main conference room), 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday 27 May, 1700-1800 (5-6:00pm).  This month there will be a guest speaker (via Big Blue Button), Todd Cole, who is the Team Leader of the Ubuntu Arizona Local Community Team (AZLOCO).  His subject will be ProxMox.  As usual you can also bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them.  I encourage you to bring a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux and FOSS.

See you there.

Rex Bouwense
