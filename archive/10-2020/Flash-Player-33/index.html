Flash Player

From Daniel Thomas · Oct 17, 2020

Flash-based content will be blocked from running in Adobe Flash Player after December 31st, the EOL Date for Adobe support of Flash Player.

For current Linux systems this will impact at least the following:

  • YUM, TAR.GZ, RPM and APT packages for NPAPI and PPAPI

  • Latest versions of Firefox and Google Chrome  

What about other applications I may have downloaded?

I know developers were warned 3 years ago that this day was coming but what should a novice like myself do, if anything.
