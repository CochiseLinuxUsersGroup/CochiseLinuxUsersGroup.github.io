CLUG Meeting on 24 October

From Rex Bouwense · Oct 04, 2020

Hello all,
We have obtained a meeting location for the 24 October CLUG meeting.  It is 143 Street Tacos, 80 S. Carmichael, Sierra Vista.  Of course they serve food (vegan selection is available as well).  They have an excellent meeting room in the back equipped with a projector and TV and space for 28 people so we can eat before, during, and after the meeting which will start at 1300 and run until 1545 or until we run out of things to do.  Street Tacos is allowing us to use the room at no cost.  All they ask is for us to eat there.  In addition to Ted's presentation on "Rejuvenating Your Computer" there will be a short presentation by me on "Making a Bootable Flash Drive" and then a workshop where we will make flash drives or assist members while they make flash drives for their operating system of choice.  Therefore, bring your laptops and a blank flash drive if you want to make one or have one made.  If you don't have a laptop just bring a blank flash drive and we will help you out.

Rex Bouwense
