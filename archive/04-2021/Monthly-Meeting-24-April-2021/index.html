Monthly Meeting 24 April 2021

From Rex Bouwense · Apr 18, 2021

    The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting on Saturday 24 April at 1300 (1 pm.) at 143 Street Tacos, 80 S. Carmichael Ave., Sierra Vista.  There will be a presentation on "Robo Linux OS" by Tim Kirk followed by our usual discussion of Linux computer problems and questions by members which we do our best to answer and solve before the end of the meeting.  This meeting is significant in that it is the 100th meeting of the group so bring a family member, a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux and Free and Open Source Software. 
    As I always mention Street Tacos has a varied menu of tacos and other items for sale.  You can choose to arrive early and eat before the meeting, eat during the meeting, or stay after the conclusion of the meeting and eat with some of us who prefer to eat later.  We are not being charged for the use of the meeting room so let us patronize our host who arguably serves the best tacos in Sierra Vista and probably all of Southeastern Arizona. 

Rex Bouwense
