CLUG Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Jan 21, 2020

The Cochise Linux User Group will hold its monthly meeting on
Saturday, 25 January 2020, at 1300 in the Sierra Vista Public Library
conference room, 2600 E. Tacoma Street, Sierra Vista AZ. There will be a
presentation on "Tails OS, the anonymous Linux Operating System" by Dr.
Tim Kirk, the driving force behind the founding and running of the
Warrior Healing Center, the one stop shop for veterans and their
dependents who are having problems, here in Sierra Vista and in Cochise
County. Lets have a good showing for this presentation. Attend if you
can and bring a family member, a friend, or a co-worker to introduce
them to other aspects of Linux Operating Systems.
Don't forget the free lecture on AI vs Humans on Monday, 27
January. Please RSVP to Dr Delalla if you plan to attend this one.
Rex
