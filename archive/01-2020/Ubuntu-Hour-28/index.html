Ubuntu Hour

From Rex Bouwense · Jan 06, 2020

The Cochise Linux User Group will host a Ubuntu Hour at Schlotzsky's on
09 January from 1800-1900. While this is primarily a social event it is
also an opportunity to introduce a family member, a friend, or a
co-worker to Linux and FOSS. It is also an opportunity to mingle with
other Linux users, have questions answered, and find a solution to a
problem that has been bothering you for a while, all while enjoying a
great sandwich and a cup of coffee or a glass of soda.
Rex
