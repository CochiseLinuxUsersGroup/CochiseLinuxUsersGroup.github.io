Cochise Linux Google Group

From Rex Bouwense · Jun 18, 2022

If you accept the invitation to join the Cochise Linux Google Group, sign in to your Google account and select Google Drive from the menu.  You will notice that certain documents from the Group have been made available under "shared with me". 
The file Cochise-Linux contains three files all of which have data or information for you. 
    1.  CLUG Presentations have past presentation slides and a form where you can request future presentations or discussions on topics that are of interest to you.
    2.  Inventories has CLUG computer parts and accessories that are on hand and available to members (free of charge of course).
    3.  The Problems and Solutions file has several documents that are designed for you to present problems as well as make suggestions to improve the site and a How To Do certain things in Linux.
Look them over and feel free to make suggestions or present your problems.
Hats off to Bruce who designed the Group.  It is a great start and we are looking forward to your participation.

Rex Bouwense
