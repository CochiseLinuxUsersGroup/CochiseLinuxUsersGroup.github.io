Re: (initramis)_ instead of booting

From Charles Breaux · Jun 04, 2022

Thank you Gene and Bruce. It took me a while of wading through “fixes” that didn’t work, even some posts that claimed you can’t fix the initramfs_ with recent versions of Ubuntu. And Bruce, there wasn't an issue with lost data, because I use several computers I back up all new data to a usb drive in real time that I move from computer to computer, and then painstakingly transfer to 2 external HDs once in a while. Can't remember why I quit using time shift. And I used to also have a back up OS on an external drive that I could just copy, which I'm gonna do again after this experience.

Here is the fix that finally worked in case anyone is interested.

Actually the solution is easy just write the command of fsck /dev/sdax like the below and give Y if the console ask for fixing something:

(initramfs) fsck /dev/sda1

or

(initramfs) fsck /dev/sdaX

X specifies mounted disk part number.

If you don't want to manually press 'y' every time it asks for a fix, you can also run the command with the -y option.

(initramfs) fsck /dev/sdaX -y

take care you all, thanks again for your suggestions
