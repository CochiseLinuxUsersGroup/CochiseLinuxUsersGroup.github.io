Google Group

From Rex Bouwense · Jun 17, 2022

Bruce has done the heavy work in organizing the Cochise-Linux Google Group.  It is a place where members may carry on conversations, ask questions, have problems solved, check our inventory of computer parts and accessories to obtain some needed part or accessory, and lots more.  In the very near future you will be sent an invitation to join the Group.  While you do not need a Google account to view anything, you will need a one to comment and actively participate.  This program is not designed to replace the mailing list but rather to supplement it as well as Facebook (for those of you that have a Facebook account).  It will give members another option to communicate within the Cochise Linux User Group.  If you do not have a Google Account, it is relatively simple to set one up and you can use the email address solely for entry in to the Group if you do not want to use it for anything else.  Merely go to:
https://accounts.google.com/signup/v2/webcreateaccount?continue=https%3A%2F%2Fmyaccount.google.com%3Futm_source%3Daccount-marketing-page%26utm_medium%3Dcreate-account-button&flowName=GlifWebSignIn&flowEntry=SignUp
to create your account.
