CLUG Meeting 12 September 2018

From Rex Bouwense · Sep 09, 2018

There will be a Cochise Linux User Group meeting at the University of
Arizona South (Learning Resource Center), 1140 Colombo Ave., Sierra
Vista AZ at 5 p.m. on 12 September 2018. One of the items that we will
be discussing is the advantages and disadvantages of using a common
password to install distros at our next installfest in November and what
the fallout might be. Also on the agenda is the possibility of holding
some kind of installfest in Sunsites. Please attend if you can.

Rex Bouwense
