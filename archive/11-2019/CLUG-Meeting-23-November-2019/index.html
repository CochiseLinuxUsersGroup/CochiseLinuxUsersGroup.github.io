CLUG Meeting 23 November 2019

From Rex Bouwense · Nov 18, 2019

The Cochise Linux User Group will hold its monthly meeting at the Sierra
Vista public library conference room at 1300 on Saturday 23 November
2019. In addition to normal monthly business and discussions about
Linux problems, Gene will be giving a presentation on Clonezilla and
cloning. Attend if you can and bring a family member, friend, or
co-worker to introduce them to Linux and Free and Open Source Software.

Rex Bouwense
