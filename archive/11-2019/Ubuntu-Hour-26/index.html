Ubuntu Hour

From Rex Bouwense · Nov 11, 2019

The Cochise Linux User Group will host a Ubuntu Hour on Thursday 14
November 6-7 pm. at Schlotzsky's, 3900 E. Fry Blvd. Sierra Vista AZ.
Bring a family member, friend, or co-worker and introduce them to the
wonderful world of Linux while enjoying a great sandwich and a cup of
coffee or a soda. Interesting conversations always occur and you can
meet other Linux users. If you have a question or a problem be sure and
raise it there. The members will be more than glad to give you a
solution or at least point you in the correct direction.

Rex Bouwense
