Interesting Reading

From Rex Bouwense · Jul 08, 2020

Since many of you have some extra time on your hands, you may be interested in reading some informative articles.

The first  https://www.maketecheasier.com/which-ubuntu-flavor-should-you-choose/ is a discussion of the seven Ubuntu "flavors" and how they differ from each other.  Of course they are merely different desktop environments.  You are not  limited to the standard default gnome desktop environment.  There are others out there and they all are only released in 64 bit versions.

Next we have https://9to5linux.com/meet-ubuntued-20-04-an-educational-ubuntu-flavor-for-kids-schools-and-universities
It discusses UbuntuEd 20.04 which appears to be a revival of the old EdUbuntu which was discontinued in 2014.  The educational application bundles are Pre-school for ages <6, Primary for ages 6-12, Secondary for ages 13-18, and Tertiary for University students.

Finally we have a discussion of Linux Mint 20 and Mint's refusal to support Ubuntu's SNAP software.  https://www.zdnet.com/article/mint-20-jpglinux-mint-20-still-the-best-linux-desktop-despite-one-quirk/

I will also post these links in the minutes of tomorrow's Ubuntu Hour.  Be there for some interesting discussions and a great sandwich, (Scholtzsky's at 1800).
