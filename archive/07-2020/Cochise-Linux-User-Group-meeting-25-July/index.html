Cochise Linux User Group meeting 25 July

From Rex Bouwense · Jul 19, 2020

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting on
Saturday, 25 July at the Bisbee Senior Center, 300 Collins Road, Bisbee
1300-1545. In lieu of a presentation, we will be conducting a hands on
installation practice for those who feel that they need some practice
installing. For those of you living in Sierra Vista we will be meeting
at the Mall parking lot by the old Sears store if you want to convoy
from there or ride share. Departure time will be at 1200 so eat lunch
before you arrive. Please wear your CLUG T-shirt and hat to the
meeting. This meeting is also an attempt to expand the group's
membership in the Eastern part of the county. We will also be
introducing our Put & Take box for computer accessories and parts.
Again I want to stress that this is for fully functional
accessories/parts. If they are broken please just throw them away.

Rex Bouwense
