CLUG Meeting 23 January

From Rex Bouwense · Jan 18, 2021

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting on Saturday 23 January 1300-1600 (1 pm -3 pm) at 143 Street Tacos, 80 S. Carmichael Ave., Sierra Vista.  The presentation will be on Ventoy, a multi-boot application that you can use to store, boot, and install multiple Linux ISOs.  You can bring your laptop and a spare USB flash drive if you want to create one of your own during the demonstration.  Street Tacos arguable serve the best Tacos in Sierra Vista and possible the best in SE Arizona so bring a family member, a neighbor, a friend, or a co-worker to the meeting and enjoy some tacos before, during, or after the meeting with other members and some interesting conversation with other Linux users.  Questions and/or problems will also be addressed after the presentation so if you are having a problem with your Linux system bring it up and we will attempt to provide you with an answer or a solution or at least point you in the correct direction.

Rex Bouwense
majb@azloco.com
