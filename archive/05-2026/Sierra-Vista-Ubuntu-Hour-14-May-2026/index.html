Sierra Vista Ubuntu Hour 14 May 2026

From Rex Bouwense · May 10, 2026

The Cochise Linux User Group will host a Ubuntu Hour this Thursday, 14
May, at 1800 (6 pm.) at Schlotzsky's, 3900 East Fry Boulevard, Sierra
Vista. While it is primarily a social hour, questions are always asked
and answered, and problems are always presented and almost always
solved. Come out and enjoy a great sandwich and a soda or a cup of
coffee. Bring a family member, a co-worker, a neighbor, or a friend and
introduce them to Linux and Free and Open Source Software. Enjoy
meeting and talking to other Linux users. See you there.

Rex Bouwense
