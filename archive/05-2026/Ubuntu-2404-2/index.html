From Rex Bouwense · May 07, 2026

For Your Information, Ubuntu Unity has been and is an official "flavor" of Ubuntu.  However, the 26.04 version that was released on 23 April is not a LTS so it is only supported for 9 months.  The lead developer is dropping out of the picture to further his education.  There are people standing in line to take his place but the team has yet to apply for LTS status for Ubuntu Unity 26.04.  If you want LTS stay with 24.04.4.

Rex Bouwense
