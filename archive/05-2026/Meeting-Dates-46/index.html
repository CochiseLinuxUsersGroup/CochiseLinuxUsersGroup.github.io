Meeting Dates

From Rex Bouwense · May 02, 2026

After tabulating the responses, it is the consensus of those returning
their preferences that the meeting dates should remain on Tuesday. I
want to thank those of you that voted. See you at the next meeting
which will be on Tuesday, 26 May 2026 at the Warrior Healing Center main
conference room from 1700-1800 (5-6 pm).

We recently received an additional six computers that were destined for
the dump or recycle from Sentrillion thanks to some quick thinking on
the part of Louie Garcia. Four of them have already been cleaned,
renovated, had Ubuntu 26.04 installed on them, and are ready for
donation which brings our total of computers ready for donation to 11
desktops and one laptop, all of which have some form of Ubuntu 26.04
installed on them. If you are aware of anyone who is in need of a
computer please contact me or David Garcia for distribution.

Rex Bouwense
