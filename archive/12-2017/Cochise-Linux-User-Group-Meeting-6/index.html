Cochise Linux User Group Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Dec 10, 2017

This is a reminder that there is a meeting of the Cochise Linux User
Group at the University of Arizona South Learning Resource Center on 13
December 2017 from 5:00 to 6:00 pm. Hopefully, we will have a
discussion by David Garcia on his activities with the ASUS ChromeBook.
In addition there will be an update on the upcoming CLUG Installfest on
20 January. Rex Bouwense will have some additional computer components
available (free for anyone who wants them) after the meeting. Our CLUG
T-shirts have been ordered and have an availability date of 21
December. Additional information will be available as it is received.
We are continuing to receive free publicity announcements on the Cochise
Trading Post of our meetings, Ubuntu Hour, and other activities. The
new CLUG information/business cards have been printed and are available
from Rex Bouwense. Take as many as you need. If you want your name and
a short bio (and picture if you so desire) on the Members Page of the
Group website please submit the information to either Rex Bouwense or
David Garcia and they will take care of inserting or correcting any
inaccurate information that may already exist.

Rex Bouwense
