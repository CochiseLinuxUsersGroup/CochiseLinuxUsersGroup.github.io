Sierra Vista Ubuntu Hour

From Rex Bouwense · May 08, 2019

There will be a Ubuntu Hour at Schlotzsky's, 3900 E. Fry Blvd in Sierra
Vista on Thursday 09 May from 1800-1900. It is a social hour for Ubuntu
and other Linux users to get together and enjoy a great sandwich and a
cup of coffee or soda. Attend if you can and bring a family member,
co-worker, or friend to introduce them to the wonderful world of Linux
operating systems for their personal computer and other Free and Open
Source Software.
Rex Bouwense

Sierra Vista Ubuntu Hour

From Rex Bouwense · Feb 04, 2024

The Cochise Linux User Group will host a Ubuntu Hour this Thursday, 08
February 2024, at 1800 (6 pm.) at Schlotzski's, 3900 East Fry Boulevard,
Sierra Vista. While it is primarily a social hour, questions are always
asked and answered, and problems are always presented and almost always
solved. Come out and enjoy a great sandwich and a soda or a cup of
coffee. Bring a family member, a co-worker, a neighbor, or a friend and
introduce him or her to Linux and Free and Open Source Software. Enjoy
meeting and talking to other Linux users. See you there.

Rex Bouwense
