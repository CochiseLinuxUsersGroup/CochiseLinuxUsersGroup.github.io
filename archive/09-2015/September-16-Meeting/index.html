September 16 Meeting

From MajB · Sep 17, 2015

A meeting of the Cochise Linux User Group was held on 16 September at the University of Arizona, Sierra Vista Campus. Six members and 14 total people attended. A presentation on "Introduction to Linux" was given by Rex Bouwense. It was decided to reschedule the October and November meetings to the third Wednesday of the month (21 October and 18 November because of scheduling conflicts and Veterans Day respectively. They will be at the same time (5 pm.) and location (Room C165A North, University of Arizona, Sierra Vista campus). Starting in December the meetings will return to the second Wednesday of the month.
Rex
