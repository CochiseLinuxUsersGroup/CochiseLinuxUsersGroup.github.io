Ubuntu Hour

From Rex Bouwense · Jun 09, 2020

After two months of holding our Ubuntu Hour on line we have been given
permission to once again physically meet at Schlotzsky's. So come out
and meet with other Linux users in a relaxed atmosphere, enjoy a cup of
coffee or soft drink and a great sandwich, and talk about Linux OS's or
FOSS. Masks are of course optional and we will do our best to spread
out in the meeting room so as not to make anyone uncomfortable. The
event will start at 1800 (6 pm) and generally lasts an hour. Bring a
family member, a co-worker, a neighbor, or a friend and introduce them
to Linux and FOSS. Some of us show up early to secure the room and get
the ball rolling. As with any of our events if you have a problem or a
question, do not hesitate to ask it. I will have CLUG T-shirts and caps
for those of you who have not received yours yet.

Rex
