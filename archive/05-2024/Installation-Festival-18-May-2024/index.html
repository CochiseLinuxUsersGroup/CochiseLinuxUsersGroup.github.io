Installation Festival, 18 May 2024

From Rex Bouwense · May 15, 2024

Just a reminder that we will be conducting an Installfest this
Saturday, 18 May from 1100-1500 (11am-3pm), at the Warrior Healing
Center's main conference room, 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista. Our
main mission will be to install the new Ubuntu release (24.04) on the
computers in their computer lab (that the CLUG maintains). Secondary
mission will be to conduct installs on any CLUG owned computer that has
not yet been donated. There are 4 of them on hand. Of course we will
also handle any walk-ins that want a Linux system installed on their
computer. There will be a minimum of 10 computers that will require
installation. Hopefully we will get to them all. Come out and support
the Group. See you there.

Rex Bouwense
