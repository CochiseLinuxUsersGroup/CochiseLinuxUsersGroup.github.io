Ubuntu Hour 11 March 2021

From Rex Bouwense · Mar 07, 2021

The Cochise Linux User Group will host a Ubuntu Hour at Schlotzsky's, 3900 E. Fry Blvd. on Thursday, 11 March 1800-1900 (6-7 pm.).  While primarily a social event, the conversation always turns to Ubuntu, Linux, and Free and Open Source Software.  If you have a question or a problem bring it to the event and we will attempt to answer or solve it.  Bring a family member, a neighbor, a friend, or a co-worker and introduce them to the wonderful world of Linux and while you are at it enjoy a a great sandwich and a cup of coffee or a soda and mingle with other Linux users from the county.
Rex Bouwense
