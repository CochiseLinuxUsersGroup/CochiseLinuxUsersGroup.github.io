Monthly Group Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Mar 21, 2021

    The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting on Saturday 27 March at 1300 (1 pm.) at 143 Street Tacos, 80 S. Carmichael Ave., Sierra Vista.  There will be a presentation on Introduction To Coding by Mike followed by our usual discussion of Linux computer problems and questions by members which we do our best to answer and solve before the end of the meeting. 
    After members have had a month to think about the subject raised at the last meeting "Where do we go from here" we will again discuss it.  To grow as a group we need new members and fresh ideas.  Without those we will stagnate.  Bring your ideas and discuss them with other members  Do we need new leadership?  Are more presentations by professionals in the field needed?  If so what topics need to be covered?  Would you like to see a change in the format of the meetings?
    As I always mention Street Tacos has a varied menu of tacos and other items for sale.  You can choose to arrive early and eat before the meeting or stay after the conclusion of the meeting and eat with some of us who prefer to eat later.  We are not being charged for the use of the meeting room so let us patronize our host who arguably serves the best tacos in Sierra Vista and probably all of Southeastern Arizona.  Please do not bring food or drink into the meeting.  Bring a family member, a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux and Free and Open Source Software.

Rex Bouwense
