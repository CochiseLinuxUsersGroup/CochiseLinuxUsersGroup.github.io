Ubuntu Hour

From MajB · Sep 25, 2015

A Ubuntu Hour was held on 24 September 2015 at Schlotzsky's Cafe on Fry
Blvd from 6-9 pm. Eventually the discussion moved to the Cochise Linux
User Group since many present are also members of the CLUG. Many of
those present expressed the desire for a second monthly CLUG meeting on
a Saturday to accommodate some people who have classes during the week,
work later, or are just unable to regularly attend a weekday meeting. I
am open to the idea but felt the need to bring this request to the
entire membership for comments. We will of course discuss this at the
next regular meeting which is still scheduled for 21 Oct. Please submit
any comments that you have or bring them with you and present them at
the next meeting.
A friendly reminder, there is a University of Arizona Continuing
Education free lecture on The Internet of Things on 6 October from 5-7
pm. in room C165A North. Please attend if you can.
Rex Bouwense

Ubuntu Hour

From Rex Bouwense · Apr 03, 2020

With regret I must cancel the Ubuntu Hour scheduled for 09 April at
Schlotzsky's due to the Corona virus situation. However, since I had 8
responses to my email reference: On Line Meetings, I am going to
schedule an on line meeting for the same time 09 April 1800 hours. The
procedure to join is simple. Use your web browser to go to the Big Blue
Button log in page, https://bbb.azloco.net/. Enter your name and click
join. You will be presented with a choice of joining with your
microphone or listening only. If you select microphone please use a
headset to cut down on background noise. From there you will have an
audio test. If you hear yourself select yes. That is all. Once in you
may decide to use your webcam. To do that merely select the 3rd icon on
the bottom of the screen and the system will search for your webcam.
Once it is found select start sharing. There is no requirement to use
the webcam for those of you that do not want to do so. This will be a
very simple meeting mainly for organization and to get people used to
joining the meeting and using its various options. We will of course
answer questions and hopefully solve any problems that you might have
but no major presentation is scheduled. I will probably be on line
before the meeting starts to assist anyone having problems getting on.
Of course the CLUG IRC channel, #cochiselinux will be operational as well.
Rex
