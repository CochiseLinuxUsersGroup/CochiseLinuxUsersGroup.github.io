Cochise Linux User Group Monthly Meeting 24 February 2026

From Rex Bouwense · Feb 22, 2026

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at Warrior Healing Center's main conference room, 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday, 24 February, 1700-1830 (5-6:30pm).  There will be a presentation on "The Installation and Configuration of Virtual Box".   Four of our computers will be available to have Virtual Box installed and set up on them for any of our members who wish to practice doing that.  Of course you may bring your own computer if you wish.  There will of course be experienced individuals on hand to provide one on one assistance if required.  In addition, members are still invited to bring their Linux computer problems for assistance.  Bring a family member, a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to the Linux users of the CLUG.  Whenever we meet, conversations about Linux and FOSS always arise so bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them.

See you there.

Rex Bouwense
