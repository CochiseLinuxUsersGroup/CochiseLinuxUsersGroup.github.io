Re: Devi Garcia Spam

From Rex Bouwense · Dec 28, 2018

There are several ways to pass information to the members of the Cochise
Linux User group; the regular meetings and events of the group, our IRC
channel (which is really under-used), and the mailing list. This
information takes the form of problems or questions that a member may
have about his/her operating system or other software, announcements
about meetings or events (mainly from the leaders of the group), and
links to articles that have been written which may or may not be of
interest to some of the membership. Since we only physically meet twice
a month the mailing list allows us to stay in touch at other times. If
you do not want to receive some of these emails you are free to create a
filter to send them directly to trash or any other folder that you want
to be read at a later date.
Rex Bouwense
