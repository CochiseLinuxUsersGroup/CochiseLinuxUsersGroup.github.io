Cochise Linux User Group Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Dec 19, 2018

There is a meeting of the Cochise Linux user Group at the Sierra
Vista Public Library conference room, 2600 E. Tacoma St. on Saturday 22
December at 1300. Tom will be discussing AntiX as a possible
replacement for our "go to" light distro. If there is time Rex will be
discussing Xubuntu. Attend if you can and put your 2 cents in.
We are still looking for a place to hold our February Installfest.
There are a couple of places that have been suggested, Livia's Coffee
House and the Port Royale Club House. So if you have a preference
please contact Rex. If you have another location please let us know.
Rex Bouwense
