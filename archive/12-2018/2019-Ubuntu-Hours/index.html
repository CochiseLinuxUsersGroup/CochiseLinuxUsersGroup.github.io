2019 Ubuntu Hours

From Rex Bouwense · Dec 14, 2018

I have coordinated with Brandi, the General Manager at Schlotzsky's and
she has given her stamp of approval to our conducting our Ubuntu Hour on
the 2nd Thursday of each month in Schlotzsky's back room. So the Ubuntu
Hours for the next year will be 1800-1900 on:

10 January
14 February
14 March
11 April
09 May
13 June
11 July
08 August
12 September
10 October
14 November
12 December

The change was required to eliminate back to back events. There are now
at least 9 days between events and that only occurs 3 times in 2019.

Rex Bouwense
