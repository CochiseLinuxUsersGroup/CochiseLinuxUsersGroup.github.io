CLUG Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Nov 16, 2020

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting this Saturday, 21 November, at 1300 at 143 Street Tacos, 80 S. Carmichael Ave.  They serve what is arguably the best tacos in Sierra Vista.  Come early and eat before the meeting, eat during the meeting, or stay after the conclusion of the meeting and eat then.  There will be a presentation on "Conducting a Full Install of Ubuntu to a USB Flash Drive" after which we will have our normal give and take on Linux.  Ask your questions or present your problems and we will attempt to answer or solve them before the end of the meeting.  Bring a family member, a neighbor, a friend, or a co-worker to introduce them to Linux.  We are always looking for new members.

Rex Bouwense
majb@azloco.com
