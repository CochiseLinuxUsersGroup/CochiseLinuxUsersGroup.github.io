Cochise Linux User Group meeting

From MajB · Sep 13, 2016

***REMINDER***
There will be a meeting of the Cochise Linux User Group on 14 September,
5-6 PM, at the University of Arizona South, Sierra Vista campus. Since
our normal meeting room is being renovated we will meet in the commons
area of the Resource Learning Center which is directly across from our
normal meeting room. Look for the signs and the banner. It is
important that you attend if you can. Although there is no presentation
scheduled we will be discussing our plans for an installation
festival/Linux Workshop here in Sierra Vista, the training required for
our members before the event, and any possible changes to the meeting
schedule and locations for the next calendar year. Also the CLUG
calling cards will be passed out. They are back from the printer and
ready to be used.
Rex Bouwense
