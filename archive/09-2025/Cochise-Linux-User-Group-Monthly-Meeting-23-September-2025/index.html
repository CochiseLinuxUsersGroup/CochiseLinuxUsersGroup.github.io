Cochise Linux User Group Monthly Meeting 23 September 2025

From Rex Bouwense · Sep 21, 2025

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at the Warrior Healing Center (main conference room), 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday 23 September, 1700-1800 (5-6:00pm).  This month we will be conducting our first Linux Workshop.  You are encouraged to bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them.  If you have Linux projects that you need assistance, bring them as well.  I encourage you to bring a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux and FOSS.

See you there.

Rex Bouwense
