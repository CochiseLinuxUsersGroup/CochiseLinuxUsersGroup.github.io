Re: Calendar help

From Devi Garcia · Sep 28, 2026

The second Thursday of the month meeting is more of a social hour, whereas the 4th Tuesday of the month is either a dinner, a workshop, or a presentation meeting the 3 days a week. Mini workshops you are mentioning are usually to update computers. Sometimes install Linux on newly donated computers or just to come in and ask questions that you may have pertaining to Foss or Linux In general. There's not really a schedule of what we do or don't do on those 3 days a week, which are Monday, Wednesday, and Friday between 9:00 a.m. And 12:00 p.m.
