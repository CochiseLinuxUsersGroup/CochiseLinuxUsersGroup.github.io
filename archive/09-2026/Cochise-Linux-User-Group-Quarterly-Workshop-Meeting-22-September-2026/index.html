Cochise Linux User Group Quarterly Workshop Meeting 22 September 2026

From Rex Bouwense · Sep 20, 2026

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its quarterly workshop meeting at Warrior Healing Center's main conference room, 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday, 22 September, 1700-1800 (5-6 pm).  You are invited to bring your Linux questions and problems and hopefully an answer or solution can be provided.  As always you are asked to bring a family member, a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to the Linux users of the CLUG.  Perhaps they too will find an alternative operating system for their computer.

See you there.

Rex Bouwense
