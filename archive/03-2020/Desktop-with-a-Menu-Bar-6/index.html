Unity desktop is no longer supported by Canonical.  It is currently community supported.  Devi has previously submitted a URL reference Unity and its future when version 8 is released.  Reference your question about desktop environments:  The ones that are officially supported by Canonical are Gnome, KDE, XFCE, LXDE, LXQT, Budgie, and Mate. There are probably an addition 15-20 DE's that are not installed by default and are not officially supported as a flavor of Ubuntu.  If you are concerned about a panel taking up space on your monitor you can always decrease the size of the panel or hide it.  Ubuntu has an option in settings that will hide the Dash (which is the quick launch panel installed on the left of your screen by default).  In other DE's the option is located in preferences.  For instance In LXDE, you right click on the panel and change your options.  Each DE probably has a different way to do this.
