CLUG Meeting 28 March 2020

From Rex Bouwense · Mar 18, 2020

The Sierra Vista public library has closed its doors for eight weeks and
all organizations that had rooms reserved have been notified that their
reservation has been canceled because of the Corona Virus situation. I
hesitated to notify the membership because I had a plan B which was to
meet at the Warrior healing Center. Today they have closed their doors
until further notice. Since our Installation Festival was scheduled to
take place at the Warrior Healing Center on 04 April, we no longer have
a venue to conduct it and no longer have a venue for our 28 March Group
meeting. Therefore, I must cancel the 28 March CLUG meeting and the 04
April Installfest unless a member has a reliable venue where we may
conduct these events and makes it know in sufficient time to publicize
it. Members are encouraged to continue to use the mailing list or the
IRC channel for questions and/or problems. I will continue to monitor
the Covid-19 situation and keep the membership informed of any
additional changes.
Rex Bouwense
