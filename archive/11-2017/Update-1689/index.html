From Rex Bouwense · Nov 17, 2017

The next CLUG sponsored installfest will be on 20 January 2018, 10-4pm
at the University of Arizona South Learning Resource Center, 1140 N.
Colombo Avenue, Sierra Vista. Make plans now to attend and assist
others in installing and enjoying a Linux operating system.

We were able to reserve the Sierra Vista public library conference room
for our Saturday meetings in 2018. Therefore we will meet there on
every 4th Saturday of each month in the calendar year 2018,
1:00-3:30pm. Put the dates on your calendar and plan to attend.
