"They speak in esoteric terms and procedures that are mostly impossible for the non-geek to understand."

I've used unix decades ago.  Around 10 years ago I installed Ubuntu and did not like it.  A few years ago I installed Debian which I find pleasant to use.  However, whenever I had a problem and did a search, the answers were almost always convoluted and seemingly more complicated than necessary.  I've come to accept that that is the way it is: solving simple problems often requires following a thread of multiple searches.  So, it's not you.  But, Linux gets better all the time, so just stay with it, and bring it up at the next CLUG meeting along with your laptop.

JMHO.
