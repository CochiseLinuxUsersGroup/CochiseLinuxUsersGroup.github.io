Upcoming Events

From Rex Bouwense · Nov 21, 2017

There is a Cochise Linux User Group meeting at the Sierra Vista public
library conference room on Saturday 25 November 1-3:45pm. Attend if you
can. In addition there are three University of Arizona free lectures in
the public meeting room of Groth Hall. They are co-sponsored by the
University and IEEE of Fort Huachuca.

29 November 11:30-1pm Maker Space: Inventions and Patent Process
29 November 5:30-7pm IOT: Hardening the Internet of Things
06 December 5:30-7pm Pen Testing

Attend any or all of these presentations if you can and have an
interest. Please RSVP to Dr. John DeLalla if you plan to attend so he
can reserve a seat for you because seating is limited and fills up.
Pizza will be served.

Rex Bouwense
