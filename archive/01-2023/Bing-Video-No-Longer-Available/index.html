Bing : "Video No Longer Available"

From " · Jan 14, 2023

Greetings!

I find myself oddly in front of a screen and made a recent discovery to share. There are those times where I use the different search engines. There have been times where searching topics through Bing, a result would return video options. Occasionally clicking on the returned video would simply open a video frame with the error message, "Video No Longer Available".

This of course was a frustration and usually I would simply move on following the path of least resistance and not wasting time and more effort. However, my relaxed disposition this morning allowed me a moment of clarity. I ran across this situation and glanced at all the page elements paying attention to the referenced URL. It turns out to be an HTTP rather than HTTPS. A simple edit to make it HTTPS allowed the video to play.

Hey, who would have thought a simple edit would be a path to gratification. Sharing it to improve other's experience.

Be well and cheers!
