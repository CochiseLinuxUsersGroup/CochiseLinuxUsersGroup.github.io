Ubuntu Hour 26 April 2018

From Rex Bouwense · Apr 23, 2018

***REMINDER***
There is a Ubuntu Hour at Schlotzsky's Cafe on 26 April 2018 from 6-7
p.m. The major topic for discussion will be our Installfest on the 28th
but as what normally occurs other topics arise that are Linux, Open
Source Software, or even non IT related. This is a social hour so come
out, enjoy the company of other Linux users for an hour or so, and have
a great sandwich and a cup of coffee.
