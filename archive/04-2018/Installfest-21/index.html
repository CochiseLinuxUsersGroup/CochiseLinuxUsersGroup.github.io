Installfest

From Rex Bouwense · Apr 04, 2018

The Cochise Linux User Group will be conducting an Installfest on 28
April at the Sierra Vista Public Library Mona Bishop room, 2600 E.
Tacoma St. Sierra Vista AZ, from 1100 to 1500. I encourage you to
attend to assist in installing Linux Operating Systems on computers or
just to show your support for the Group. Installers are needed. If you
are hesitant about installing on someone else's computer, training is
available. Contact Rex Bouwense or Greg Christ. Attached is a flyer
that you are encouraged to make copies of to share with your family,
your neighbors, your friends, and your co-workers. Please post copies
at your place of worship and at your job site. Everyone is welcome.

Rex Bouwense
