21 May Installfest/Linux Workshop

From Rex Bouwense · May 16, 2022

This Saturday, 21 May the Cochise Linux User Group will conduct an Installation Festival/Linux Workshop at the Warrior Healing Center, 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista in their main conference room from 1000 to 1400 hours.  In addition to installing a LInux OS on computers we will also assist members who have a problem with their computer solve that problem.  Just bring your problem and everything you need to show us the problem (We are not very good at guessing)  to the event.  If the problem is in the computer, you must bring it.  Install media, power, and internet access are present and have been check for functionality.  While the event starts at 1000, I would appreciate assistance setting up the room so anyone that would like to show up early to set up is very welcome.

Rex
