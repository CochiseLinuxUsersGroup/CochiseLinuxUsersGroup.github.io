Re: boot fails

From Devi Garcia · May 26, 2022

Use cat /proc/partitions or blkidin Busybox to get your partition details. The fsck command should fix your problem in a matter of seconds if not a minute, what follows should be on your screen after the magic takes place.

/dev/sda1: ***** FILE SYSTEM WAS MODIFIED *****
/dev/sda1: 497733/30531584 files (0.5% non-contiguous), ........

If everything goes as planned reboot and you’re good to go by typing in “reboot” or “exit” then enter.
