Hi Charles.  Have you considered booting off of a live image and working on the drive that way?  This way you may be able to back up some of your data if you have an external hard drive you can connect, once you can get your drive fsck'd and mounted.  You also would not need to work within BusyBox.

The only time I have seen this is when a hard drive physically began failing.  That's not to say this is the case, just my experience with this.
