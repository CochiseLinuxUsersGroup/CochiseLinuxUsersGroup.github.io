CLUG Monthly meeting

From Rex Bouwense · May 26, 2022

The Cochise Linux User Group will hold its monthly meeting at 143 Street Tacos, 80 S. Carmichael, 1300-1500 (1-3 p.m.) this Saturday 28 May.  Bring your computer problems and questions and we will attempt to solve them.  Street Tacos arguably serves the best tacos in Sierra Vista and possibly in all of Southeastern Arizona so come early and enjoy some or have some during the meeting.  Bring a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux and FOSS.  See you there.
Rex Bouwense
