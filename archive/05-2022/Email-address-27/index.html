Email address

From Rex Bouwense · May 06, 2022

We recently had a member send an email to the wrong email address in an attempt to post a message to our list.  This resulted in the email being bounced not an attempted posting.  If you want to post to the members of the cochiselinux list, you must send it to cochiselinux@freelists.org.  Just be careful when addressing your email to the list.

Rex Bouwense
