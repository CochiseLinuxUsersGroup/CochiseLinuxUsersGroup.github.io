12 May Ubuntu Hour Instructions

From Rex Bouwense · May 10, 2022

    Remember to bring your laptop and headset (if you have one) to the Ubuntu Hour.  We will attempt to have both Big Blue Button and Zoom operational there.
    Big Blue Button will be operational at 1800 hour (6 pm.)  There is nothing to install when you use Big Blue Button.  Merely go to https://bbb.azloco.net, enter your name (first name is sufficient), select join, and then select how you would like to join the audio (microphone or listen only).  If you choose microphone, you will be directed to an echo test.  If you hear your voice, select yes.  You will then be connected to Big Blue Button.  Please use a headset to prevent feedback.
    The Zoom meeting will start at 1815 hours (6:15 pm.).  If you do not have the Zoom client installed, fear not.  You should be allowed to join from your web browser.  I previously sent all of you an invitation to join the Zoom meeting.  From that email select the very long URL in the message.  Your browser should open up with that address.  Scroll down and near the bottom of the page you will see "join from your browser".  Click that link and you will be directed to the meeting.
    If you are unable to attend the Ubuntu Hour feel free to join us via Big Blue Button and later Zoom from your abode.

Rex Bouwense
