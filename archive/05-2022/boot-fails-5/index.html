This would be my suggestion.  If you can’t read all the boot error message, try substituting another monitor.  Then try to reboot.  The different monitor may be able to display the message better.

 

After the boot attempt fails, you should be at the boot loader.  Try entering the commands Devi suggested.  I can’t guarantee that the commands he suggested are intended for the boot loader, but that is my guess.  Anyway, if the boot loader doesn’t recognize the commands, they will fail, and I think no harm will be done from trying them.  Or, if you don’t feel comfortable with the above suggestion, wait until one of the experts from the CLUG can give you advice that you are more comfortable with.

 

Gene
