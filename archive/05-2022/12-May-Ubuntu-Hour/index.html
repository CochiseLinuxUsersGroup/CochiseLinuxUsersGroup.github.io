12 May Ubuntu Hour

From Rex Bouwense · May 07, 2022

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct an Ubuntu Hour on Thursday 12 May at Schlotzsky's, 3900 E. Fry Blvd from 1800-1900.  While primarily a social hour to meet other Linux users, discussions about Linux operating systems, Free and Open Source Software, and of course Ubuntu always arise.  Member problems are discussed and solutions are offered.  Everyone is welcome so bring a family member, a neighbor, or a co-worker to introduce them to the world of Linux.  Enjoy a great sandwich and a cup of coffee while meeting other Linux users and perhaps having problems solved or questions answered. We will be setting up a Zoom meeting at the event as well as a Big Blue Button meeting so bring your laptops to participate.  See you there.

Rex Bouwense
