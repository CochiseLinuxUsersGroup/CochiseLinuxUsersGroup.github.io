Upcoming Events for the remainder of 2025

From Rex Bouwense · Aug 17, 2025

Since we have made some changes to our schedule, below is a list of
events that are currently scheduled for the remainder of 2025.

26 August, 1700-1800, CLUG regular meeting, Warrior Healing Center main
conference room, presentation by David Garcia on "Using Aliases"

11 September, 1800-1900, Sierra Vista Ubuntu Hour, Schlotzski's
(location subject to change)

23 September, 1700-1800, CLUG Linux Workshop, Warrior Healing Center
main conference room. Bring your problems to get solutions. Learn
about Linux.

09 October, 1800-1900, Sierra Vista Ubuntu Hour, Schlotzski's (location
subject to change)

28 October, Supper meeting at La Casita, bring the family and enjoy some
socializing with other Linux users and their family

13 November, 1800-1900, Sierra Vista Ubuntu Hour, Schlotzski's (location
subject to change)

25 November, 1700-1800, CLUG regular meeting, Warrior Healing Center
main conference room, presentation by Rex Bouwense on "Using VMs"

11 December, 1800-1900, Sierra Vista Ubuntu Hour, Schlotzski's (location
subject to change)

23 December, 1700-1800, CLUG Linux Workshop, Warrior Healing Center main
conference room. Bring your problems to get solutions. Learn about Linux.

The supper meetings are designed for you to bring your family (and
friends if desired) to socialize with other Linux users and their families.

The Linux workshops are designed for you to bring your problems to get
answers/solutions and for you to learn a bit more about Linux. Even if
you have no current problems you should attend to assist others with
their problems and to perhaps learn a bit more about Linux.

Both of these changes have been made recently and I encourage you to
take part in the events.

There has been some talk at recent events concerning moving the location
of the Ubuntu Hour. This discussion will continue at future events
until the matter is decided one way or another.

Rex
