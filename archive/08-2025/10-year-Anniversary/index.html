10 year Anniversary

From Rex Bouwense · Aug 21, 2025

Yesterday the Cochise Linux User Group celebrated its 10 year
anniversary. Since we reorganized and renovated the group in 2015, we
have conducted 300 installs on desktop and laptop computers and donated
198 computers to individuals and organizations. Job well done guys and
gals. This could not have been done with each and everyone of you.
Thank you.

Rex
