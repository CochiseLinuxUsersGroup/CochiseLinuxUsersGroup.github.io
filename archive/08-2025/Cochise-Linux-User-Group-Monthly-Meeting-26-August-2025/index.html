Cochise Linux User Group Monthly Meeting 26 August 2025

From Rex Bouwense · Aug 24, 2025

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at the Warrior Healing Center (main conference room), 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday 26 August, 1700-1800 (5-6:00pm).  This month's presentation will be "How to use Aliases" by David Garcia.  As usual you can also bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them.  I encourage you to bring a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux and FOSS.

See you there.

Rex Bouwense
