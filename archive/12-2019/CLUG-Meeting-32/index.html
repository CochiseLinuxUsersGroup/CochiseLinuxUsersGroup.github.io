CLUG Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Dec 26, 2019

There is a meeting of the Cochise Linux User Group on Saturday, 28
December at the Sierra Vista Public Library conference room at 1300. A
presentation on The Genealogy of Linux Distributions will be given.
Bring a family member, a friend, or a co-worker and introduce them to
Linux and Free and Open Source Software. If you have a problem with
your Linux operating system be sure and raise it there. We will attempt
to solve any problems and answer any questions you may have. Santa will
have come and gone and you will probably be ready for a break. Spend it
with us.
Rex Bouwense
