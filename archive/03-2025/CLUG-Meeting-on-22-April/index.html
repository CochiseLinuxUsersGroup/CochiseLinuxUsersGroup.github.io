CLUG Meeting on 22 April

From Rex Bouwense · Mar 25, 2025

After some serious discussion, it has been decided to forgo the regular
April meeting and substitute it with a gathering at The Native Grill and
Wings, 3950 Martin Luther King Jr. Pkwy. to enjoy supper with other
Linux users. The local menu and prices can be found at
https://www.allmenus.com/az/sierra-vista/622640-native-grill-wings/menu/.
You are encouraged to bring your wife (or girl friend). Bring a friend
or neighbor if you want. I am asking you to RSVP. For those of you
that do not know what that means, please respond to this email with a
yes I am coming with so many guests or no I will not be attending. This
will enable us to reserve a large enough area to accommodate our group.
While discussion about Linux topics is not forbidden it will be
discouraged to make those attending feel at ease. We really want this
to be a social event. I will be sending additional emails concerning
this event.

Rex Bouwense
