Cochise Linux User Group Monthly Meeting 25 March 2025

From Rex Bouwense · Mar 23, 2025

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at the Warrior Healing Center (main conference room), 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday 25 February, 1700-1800 (5-6:00pm).  Rex will be conducting a presentation on installing Ventoy to a flash drive (to have your very own install media).

Bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them. 

Bring a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux and FOSS.
   
See you there.
