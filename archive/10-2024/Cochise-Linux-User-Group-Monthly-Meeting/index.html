Cochise Linux User Group Monthly Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · May 24, 2024

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at the Warrior Healing Center (main conference room), 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday 28 May, 1700-1800 (5-6:00pm).  There will be a presentation on "What is new in Ubuntu 24.04" by Rex Bouwense and perhaps a small surprise from David Garcia.

Bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them. 
Bring a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux and FOSS.   
See you there.

Rex Bouwense

Cochise Linux User Group Monthly Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Oct 20, 2024

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at the Warrior Healing Center (main conference room), 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday 22 October, 1700-1800 (5-6:00pm).  I will be conducting an encore presentation on Ventoy.  It will be followed by a hands on Ventoy workshop where you can create your own Ventoy flash drive to place multiple ISOs for your personal use.  If you want to create a Ventoy flash drive bring your laptop (if you have one) and an empty (or soon to be empty because we will write over whatever is already on it) flash drive (16 GBs or larger).

Bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them. 

Bring a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux and FOSS.
   
See you there.

Rex Bouwense
