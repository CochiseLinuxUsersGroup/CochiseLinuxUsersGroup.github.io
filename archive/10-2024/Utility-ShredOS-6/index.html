What is ShredOS?

ShredOS is a USB bootable (BIOS or UEFI) small linux distribution
with the sole purpose of securely erasing the entire contents of your
disks using the program nwipe. If you are familiar with dwipe from
DBAN then you will feel right at home with ShredOS and nwipe. What
are the advantages of nwipe over dwipe/DBAN? Well as everybody
probably knows, DBAN development stopped in 2015 which means it has
not received any further bug fixes or support for new hardware since
that date. Nwipe originally was a fork of dwipe but has continued to
have improvements and bug fixes and is now available in many Linux
distros. ShredOS hopefully will always provide the latest nwipe on a
up to date Linux kernel so it will support modern hardware.
