Cochise Linux User Group meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Nov 18, 2018

There is a CLUG meeting on Saturday, 24 November 2018 in the Sierra
Vista Public Library conference room, 2600 E. Tacoma St., at 1300 (1:00
p.m.). There will be a live installation of Bodhi Linux followed by a
discussion. Bodhi is one of the candidates to become our "go to" light
distro to install on those computers that are under powered or do not
have sufficient RAM to use one of the other distributions. Volunteers
are still needed to download and demonstrate some of the other
candidates. We will also be discussing the possibility of reducing the
number of group meetings from twice a month to once a month. Attend if
you are able.

Rex
