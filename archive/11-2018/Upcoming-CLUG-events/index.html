Upcoming CLUG events

From Rex Bouwense · Nov 11, 2018

There is a Cochise Linux User Group meeting at the University of Arizona
South on 14 November at 5 p.m. Among the topics to be discussed will be
the CLUG After Action Report, a possible change of our "go to" light
distribution that we use at our installfests, the continued search for
an alternate meeting location, and the production of a CLUG Code of Conduct.

There will be a Ubuntu Hour at Schlotzsky's Cafe on 15 November at 6
p.m. This is a change from our normal 4th Thursday of the month event
because of Thanksgiving falling on the 4th Thursday of November.

Rex
