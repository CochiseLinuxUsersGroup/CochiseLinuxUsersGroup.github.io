    Once again I want to remind the subscribers of the mailing list
that if you have a question that you would like to ask or a problem to
which you are seeking a solution, please do not reply to some other
posting.  Start your own thread with a title that is recognizable. 
Explain your problem in sufficient detail that others do not have to ask
unnecessary questions of you.  That will only delay a solution.  Also
please include what you have already done in an effort to resolve the
problem.  Answers to many questions can be found by doing a simple web
search with your favorite search engine, Google or my favorite
DuckDuckGo.  If you are offering a solution or asking the original
poster a question, please reply to his/her posting.  That way we can
keep all of the related comments in one thread.
     If you find a URL that has been posted particularly interesting or
want to make a comment about it please reply to it.  The URLs that are
posted are for your information.  If you find them helpful please say so.
     I do encourage you to bring your problems and questions to one of
our meetings where members, many of which have much more knowledge and
experience with Linux operating systems than I do, may be able to
provide you with assistance or as a minimum a starting point from which
you can find what you are looking for.
     Thank you.

Rex Bouwense
