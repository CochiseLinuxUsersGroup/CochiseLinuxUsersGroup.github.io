CLUG Meeting 19 December 2020

From Rex Bouwense · Dec 13, 2020

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at 143 Street Tacos, 80 S. Carmichael Ave. in Sierra Vista on 19 December 2020 at 1300.  This is a change from our normal 4th Saturday meeting date because of a possible conflict with the Christmas holiday period.  There will be a short presentation and discussion on how to test distros without downloading them for hardware compatibility and visual compatibility with your particular likes and dislikes.  This will be followed by a presentation and discussion of the application Rescuezilla, which can be used to back up your hard drive and restore it.  Street Tacos has an excellent menu and serves arguably the best tacos in the Sierra Vista area.  They also have vegan selections.  You can come early and eat before the meeting or stay with us after the meeting has concluded and enjoy the company of other Linux users for a meal and additional conversation after the meeting has concluded.  I encourage you to wear your CLUG T-shirts and hats.  Bring a family member, a friend, or a co-worker to introduce them to the wonderful world of Linux and Free and Open Source Software.

Rex Bouwense
