What I Miss About Unity

From Eliter · Dec 14, 2020

When switching from Ubuntu Desktop 16.04 to Ubuntu Desktop 18.04 and 20.04, I have to give up Unity. Now, I'm fine with Unity, if I can get the following:
* Screenshot options after hitting "PRINT SCREEN" key on keyboard
* Exiting/minimizing maximizing button on top-left corner
* Application options on top bar (i.e. "file", "edit", "help" are common menus)
* Control + alt + num pad to move windows on monitor (ctl+alt+9 moves application to the top-right of the screen, ctl+alt+6 moves application to right of the screen, ctl+alt+5 maximizes window)

If I can get those 4 items, I think I will be VERY happy with Gnome. Gnome wastes more vertical screen real estate than Unity--why does every window need its own bar, and the OS gives you a useless drop-down menu at the top?

Also, sorry I haven't made it to the meetings.

Thanks,
Eli D.
