CLUG Ubuntu Hour 10 December 2020

From Rex Bouwense · Dec 06, 2020

The Cochise Linux User Group will host a Ubuntu Hour on 10 December at 1800 at Schlotzsky's, 3900 E. Fry Blvd. in Sierra Vista.  While this is primarily a social event, some serious discussions have been know to occur during the hour.  Linux and FOSS questions have been asked and answered.  Linux and FOSS problems have been raised and generally solved within the hour.  So take an hour out your very busy schedule and join us at the Ubuntu Hour, where you can also enjoy an excellent sandwich and a cup of coffee or a soda and visit with other Linux users.  Better yet, bring a family member, a neighbor, a co-worker, or a friend and introduce them to Linux and FOSS.
Rex Bouwense
