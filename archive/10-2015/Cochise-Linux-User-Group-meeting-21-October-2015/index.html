From MajB · Oct 18, 2015

Just a friendly reminder that there will be a Cochise Linux User
Group meeting on 21 October 2015 in Classroom C165A North, University Of

by University of Arizona South Continuing Education and IEEE Fort
Huachuca Section on "Engineering Careers in the Electrical Utility

presentations have been standing room only. Please attend if you can.
Additional CLUG/FOSS/Linux future activities:

1. 22 October 6-7 pm., Sierra Vista Ubuntu Hour (attachment 2)
Schlotzsky's Cafe, 3900 East Fry Blvd.

2. 04 November 5-7 pm., University of Arizona South Continuing
Education free lecture, US-China Cyber Relations (attachment 3)
Classroom C165A North (RSVP to John Delalla to ensure a seat).

3. 07 November 2-4 pm., Cochise Linux User Group meeting, Sierra Vista
public library conference room, 2600 East Tacoma St.
