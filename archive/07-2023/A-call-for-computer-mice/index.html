A call for computer mice

From Rex Bouwense · Jul 15, 2023

While we have 6 desktop computers ready for donation and plenty of
keyboards and monitors, we are lacking mice to go with them. If you have
any spare ones laying around in your closet, garage, or home office, I
would urge you to donate them so they could be used with the computers
that we donate. They can be dropped off at the Warrior Healing Center
computer lab.

Thank you.

Rex Bouwense
