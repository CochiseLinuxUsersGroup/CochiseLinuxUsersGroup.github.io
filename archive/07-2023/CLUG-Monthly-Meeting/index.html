CLUG Monthly Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Jul 18, 2021

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at 143 Street Tacos, 80 S. Carmichael St., Sierra Vista AZ at 1300-1500 24 July 2021.  All are welcome so bring a family member, a friend, or a co-worker to introduce them to Linux.  Street Tacos serves arguably the best tacos in Sierra Vista and possibly Southern Arizona so come early and enjoy some excellent food or eat during or after the meeting.  If you have a question or a problem be sure and raise it at the meeting at we will attempt to solve it for you before the end of the meeting.  There will be a presentation on some additional terminal commands, specifically how to change permissions and ownership of files and directories.  We will also be discussing the possibility of changing the time and/or date of our monthly meetings in an effort to increase the attendance.  Your input is encouraged.

Rex Bouwense

CLUG Monthly Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Jul 22, 2023

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at the Warrior Healing Center (main conference room), 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday, 25 July, 1700-1830 (5-6:30pm).  There will be two short presentations by Rex Bouwense and Greg Christ on the operating systems chosen by them (Sparky Linux and Voyager respectively) to hi-lite some different operating systems for the members.  Two new people will be given the laptops to continue the project for the next meeting on 22 August.  We will also be discussing a propose Kiosk Project.

Bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them. 
Bring a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux and FOSS.   
See you there.

Rex Bouwense
