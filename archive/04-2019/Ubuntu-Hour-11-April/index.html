Ubuntu Hour 11 April

From Rex Bouwense · Apr 06, 2019

There is a Ubuntu Hour at Schlotzsky's, 3900 E. Fry Blvd, Sierra Vista
on 11 April 1800-1900. This is primarily a social hour but discussions
about Ubuntu and other Linux operating systems as well as Free and Open
Source Software do occur. We have even done an installation at one of
these events. Problems and questions are raised and solutions and
answers are presented. Please attend if you can and bring a friend to
introduce him/her to Linux and FOSS. Enjoy a cup of coffee or a glass or
soda with a great sandwich while you are mingling with other Linux users.
Rex Bouwense
