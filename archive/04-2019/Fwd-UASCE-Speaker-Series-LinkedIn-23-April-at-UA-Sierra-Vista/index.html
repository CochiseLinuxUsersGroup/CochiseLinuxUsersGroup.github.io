Most of us have dabbled with creating a LinkedIn profile, yet do we know how
to set it up for the best professional results?
On 23 April at UA Sierra Vista a LinkedIn expert will present some best
practices for how to setup your profile, and we'll have a photographer
available to take headshots as well. Come dressed to professionally for if
you'd like a headshot. The talk and headshot (sent via email after the
event) are both free, thanks to the local section of IEEE.

Details:
LinkedIn: Effective Use for Professionals
Learn best practices to get the most out of this powerful professional
networking tool.
What's the best way to use LinkedIn, knowing that recruiters and potential
employers use it as a sourcing and vetting tool? In a 60-minute talk with
Q&A learn from a professional how to create a professional yet personal
profile to appropriately present yourself. Ideal for professionals and
students, this talk is geared towards those that need to create - or improve
- their LinkedIn profile.

BONUS: A photographer will be available for free headshots - dress to
impress!

Speaker:
Sami Gardner is an experienced educator who has spent the last five years
researching, teaching, and speaking on career development, branding, and
LinkedIn. Every day professionals and Silicon Valley startups have utilized
her diverse skills and insights into the job market, LinkedIn marketing, and
professional branding. She has trained job seekers, freelancers, and
companies on how to use LinkedIn at international conferences and speaking
engagements in Lisbon, Berlin, Los Angeles, and Medellin.
