From Rex Bouwense · Apr 12, 2019

In addition to the Cochise Linux User Group, there exists one other computer group in Sierra Vista and two in Tucson.  The one in SV is called Mountain View Computer users Group http://www.mvcug.org/.  The two in Tucson (that I am aware of) are the Tucson Computer Society, Linux SIG http://www.aztcs.org/sigs/linux.shtml and the Tucson Free UNIX Group http://www.tfug.org/.  The TFUG was mainly a mailing list but they lost their host and I believe that they have disbanded.  Not sure about that.  The other two have monthly physical meetings.  The proposed Fort Huachuca IEEE Computer Society may or may not have separate meetings from our own meetings and will exist as a subordinate of the Fort Huachuca IEEE Branch.  More information can be obtained by contacting Professor John DeLalla at the University of Arizona South jd@arizona.edu.
Rex Bouwense
