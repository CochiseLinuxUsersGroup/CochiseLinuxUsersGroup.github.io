Cochise Linux User group Meeting

From Rex Bouwense · Apr 22, 2019

There is a meeting of the Cochise Linux User Group on 27 April,
1300-1545 at the Sierra Vista Public Library conference room, 2600 E.
Tacoma St., Sierra Vista AZ. A presentation on "The Untold Story of
Computers" will be given by Edward (Ted) Janecki. Since the title of
the presentation has changed at least twice we are not sure what will be
covered so come out and enjoy. The newly edited/revised website will
also be revealed (if you haven't been to it recently) as will final
preparations for the Group Installfest on 4 May 1100-1500 at Livia's
Coffee, 2480 E. Wicox Drive. Bring a family member and/or a friend to
introduce them to Linux and Free and Open Source Software. See you there.
Rex Bouwense
