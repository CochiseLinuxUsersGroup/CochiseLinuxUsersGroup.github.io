Minor bug in Xubuntu

From Rex Bouwense · Jul 15, 2022

I failed to include a minor bug in the Xubuntu install media (in the
minutes) that was discussed at the Ubuntu Hour last evening. After you
have finished installing Xubuntu or completed testing it and you have
selected reboot, the familiar "remove the install media and press enter"
message does not appear on the monitor screen. There is merely a
blinking cursor in the left hand corner of the screen. So. remove the
media without receiving the message and press enter. This only happens
in Xubuntu. The message is received in all of the other flavors of
Ubuntu, Linux Mint, MX Linux, and AntiX.

Rex Bouwense
