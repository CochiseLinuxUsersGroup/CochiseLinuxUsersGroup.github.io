Cochise Linux User Group Quarterly Supper Meeting 27 October 2026

From Rex Bouwense · Oct 25, 2026

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its quarterly supper meeting at La Casita Mexican Restaurant and Cantina, 465 E. Fry Blvd, this Tuesday, 28 July, 1700-1800 (5-6 pm).  Since this is a quarterly supper meeting bring a family member, a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to the Linux users of the CLUG without having too much nerdy stuff going on.  

See you there.

Rex Bouwense
