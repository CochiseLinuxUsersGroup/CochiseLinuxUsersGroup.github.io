[meeting notes] CLUG Supper Meeting 2026-10-27

From Devi Garcia · Oct 27, 2026

Attendees:

There were 7 attendees.
Upcoming CLUG events:

12 November, Ubuntu Hour, 1800-1900, Carl’s Jr, 2080 E. Fry Blvd.
24 November, CLUG Meeting, 1700-1830, Warrior Healing Center main
conference room, 1838 Paseo San Luis
10 December, Ubuntu Hour, 1800-1900, Carl’s Jr, 2080 E. Fry Blvd.
22 December, CLUG Linux Workshop, 1700-1830, Warrior Healing Center
main conference room, 1838 Paseo San Luis
14 January, Ubuntu Hour, 1800-1900, Carl’s Jr, 2080 E. Fry Blvd.
26 January, CLUG Supper Meeting, Chili's, 4001 E. Hwy 90
Useful CLUG links:

CLUG website: https://cochiselinuxusergroup.org
CLUG FaceBook Page: https://www.facebook.com/groups/1059259857477393/
CLUG Mailing List Archives: https://www.freelists.org/archive/cochiselinux
CLUG inventory of computers and spare parts & accessories:
https://cochiselinuxusergroup.org/inventory
Announcements:

To date we have donated 263 computers to individuals or groups. This
year we have donated 42.
To date we have conducted 430 installs on laptop and desktop
computers. This year we have conducted 89.
We have 41 people on our mailing list and 60 people on our Facebook page.
We have 7 desktop computers and 2 laptop computers ready for donation.
Future Presentations:

24 November, “How to prepare Linux install media for your computer” by
Rex Bouwense
TBD, “LaTex & Texstudio” by George Self
Discussion/Problems/Questions/Activities:

All attending had a good meal and very little of the conversation was
even remotely connected with Linux or Free and Open Source Software.
However, we did briefly discuss the likelihood of electing a new
leader for the group (several names were tossed about – Peter, Tommy,
and David seemed to gather the most attention) and Linux Mint’s new
release cycle as well as Rex's inability to get the month and date
entered correctly in an email.
