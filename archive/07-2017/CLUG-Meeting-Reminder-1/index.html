CLUG Meeting Reminder

From Rex Bouwense · Jul 10, 2017

***REMINDER***

There is a meeting of the Cochise Linux user Group on Wednesday 12 July
2017 at 1700 at the University of Arizona South, Sierra Vista campus,
1140 N. Colombo Ave, Sierra Vista. We will be meeting in the Learning
Resource Center. This will be our last chance to iron out any last
minute details before our second Installation Festival which will be
held in the same location on Saturday 15 July 2017 1000-1600. If you
still have questions or are in need of a little refresher you can get
them answered on Wednesday. Please attend both if you can. We are
still in need of installers for the installfest so contact Rex or Greg
if you can participate.

Rex Bouwense
