Cochise Linux User Group Monthly Meeting 17 December 2024

From Rex Bouwense · Dec 15, 2024

The Cochise Linux User Group will conduct its monthly meeting at the Warrior Healing Center (main conference room), 1838 Paseo San Luis, Sierra Vista, this Tuesday 17 December, 1700-1800 (5-6:00pm). This date is of course a change from our normal 4th Tuesday meeting schedule because of a conflict with Christmas eve.  Doug Barth will be conducting a presentation on Back-up Alternatives.

Bring your computer problems or questions and we will attempt to solve or answer them. 

Bring a friend, a neighbor, or a co-worker and introduce them to Linux and FOSS.
   
See you there.

Rex Bouwense
