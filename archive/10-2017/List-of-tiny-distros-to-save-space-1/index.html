Thanks. FYI, I have used Puppy Linux and the LXDE edition of WattOS
(which is slightly larger than the microwatt edition.) Both are quite
small and do not require a great deal of resources to run. Puppy is best
run from a thumb drive although it can be installed on a hard drive. I
did install WattOS to a hard drive and it was relatively easy to
operate. I don't believe that Damn Small Linux is currently in
development or has been for a while.
Rex Bouwense
