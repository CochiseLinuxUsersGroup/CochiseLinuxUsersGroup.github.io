Free lectures at the UA South

From Rex Bouwense · Oct 25, 2017

I just received notification from Professor John DeLalla at the UA South
that There are six free technical guest speakers coming to campus in the
next six
weeks plus one in January. The topics are varied and you are welcome to
attend any or all of them. Flyers will be available later this week.
More information will be distributed as it becomes available. If you
want to attend please contact Dr DeLalla, jd@arizona.edu, to reserve a seat.

01 November, 5:30-7pm: Viterbi's Algorithm and Trellis Trace Back
09 November, 5:30-7pm: Cyber's Crystal Ball: Peering into the Future of IT
16 November, noon-1pm: EMC: Military Electromagnetic Compatibility
29 November 11:30am-1pm: MakerSpace: Inventions and Patent Process
29 November, 5:30-7pm: IOT: Hardening the Internet of Things
06 December, 5:30-7pm: PenTesting
31 January, 5:30-7pm: Cybersecurity from a Business Lawyers Perspective

Rex Bouwense
