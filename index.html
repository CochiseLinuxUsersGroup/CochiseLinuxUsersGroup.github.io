Cochise Linux User Group (CLUG) - Free Linux Support in Sierra Vista, Cochise County, Arizona

About Us

We're a volunteer organization in Sierra Vista & Cochise County — meeting since 2015. We help all skill levels try Linux, troubleshoot together, host free FOSS presentations from members and guest pros, and refurbish donated PCs (258 computers donated) with Ubuntu, Linux Mint and LMDE for neighbors in need. Free, friendly, no experience needed.

  • 258 computers donated
  • 419 installs completed
  • Free & open to the public

Monthly Meetings

Join us twice a month — free and open to everyone. Main Meeting: 4th Tuesday, 5pm, Warrior Healing Center, 1838 Paseo San Luis — presentation + hands-on troubleshooting. Ubuntu Hour: 2nd Thursday, 6pm, Carl's Jr., 2080 E Fry Blvd — relaxed Q&A for any Linux question. Bring your laptop; we'll help on the spot. See locations & times.

Our Goals

Linux Awareness

Try Linux without installing — we demo live USBs of Ubuntu and Mint and show free alternatives like LibreOffice and Firefox you can use on Windows or Mac today. See what we support →

Linux Support

Free, friendly help — installs, Wi-Fi, printers, and everyday use. Bring your PC and USB to our quarterly Workshop and leave with Linux running. We also have free refurbished computers & parts for members.

Linux Education

Monthly topics from Filesystem Explained to sysadmin basics — free, open to all, and recap'd in Meeting Notes and our Presentations archive.
